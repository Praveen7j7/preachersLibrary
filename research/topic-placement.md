# Topic placement: candidates against the batch criteria

Written 28 Oct 2026 under `research/placement-brief.md`. The conditions, motives and moods used here are the ones written down in
`research/batch-criteria.md` (read that first). Every proposal below is **Proposed — Not Yet Part of the Existing IYFD Catalogue** and
needs the head's approval; no existing topic is moved or renamed (rule 4).

**Candidates considered:** 117 decks (`research/iyf-materials/iyf-decks/catalogue.csv`; text extracted for #3, #4, #6, #8 and the
Happiness modules), 8 VOICE courses (`research/voice/courses.md`), 15 proposed topics (`docs/stage2-content-architecture.md` §7.1) = **140**.
Where a deck's title is unclear and no text exists, the row says so; such decks are never placed on the title alone except #114, which is
placed on IYF's own VL3 motive and flagged for confirmation.

**Decision rules applied** (brief, rules 2–3): PLACE only when the candidate matches the batch's conditions, motive **and** mood and adds
something the batch lacks; LINK when it duplicates or nearly duplicates an existing topic (it becomes material for that page); otherwise
NOT PLACED with one reason. Two reasons beyond the brief's four were needed: *held for BVS* (3 grhastha decks; BVS is left out) and
*held for TRD* (advanced material that waits for the head's TRD contents). A deck can be a **source** of a placed topic and still be linked
to an existing page (#67, #87); #41 serves two placed topics.
