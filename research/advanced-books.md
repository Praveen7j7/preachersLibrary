# Advanced Gaudiya books: which go to TRD, 29 Oct 2026

The head (29 Oct): the Sat-sandarbhas and every other advanced Gaudiya siddhanta book that no lower batch takes are
studied at **TRD**, since temple residents are at the highest stage. This sorts the ISKCON Desire Tree "Gaudiya_Books"
section (273 files, about 45 authors; catalogue `research/edt-ebooks/index.md`) plus the head's own books folder.

**Rule for every group:** a page's principle always comes from Srila Prabhupada's books; these texts add depth. Check
each edition's translator before quoting (the Desire Tree editions vary). Downloaded = text in `research/books/`.

## 2. Confidential rasa literature: the preacher's background only, never a lecture topic

Ujjvala-nilamani (and Visvanatha's Kirana), Vidagdha-madhava, Lalita-madhava, Dana-keli-kaumudi, Dana-keli-cintamani,
Govinda-lilamrta, Krsna-bhavanamrta, Gita-govinda, Radha-rasa-sudha-nidhi, Prema-samputa, the asta-kaliya-lila and
smarana texts, Surata-kathamrta, Camatkara-candrika, Radha-Krsna-ganoddesa-dipika, Gopala-campu, Krsna-karnamrta, Nisikant
Sanyal's *The Erotic Principle and Unalloyed Devotion*.

Srila Prabhupada did not lecture from these to general audiences, and warned against discussing Radha-Krishna's
intimate pastimes before a devotee is qualified. **Head, 29 Oct:** rasa and the mellows ARE TRD lecture topics (TRD
groups F–G: trd-33, trd-41 to trd-48), taught through Prabhupada's presentation (NOD, CC, Krsna book) with BRS and
Ujjvala-nilamani for depth, all `sensitive: true` and reviewed by the head. The intimate lila texts in this list stay
the preacher's background there, never quoted at length in a talk.

**Head, 29 Oct (later):** *Gopala-campu* and similar advanced books (Govinda-lilamrta, Krsna-bhavanamrta,
Radha-Krsna-ganoddesa-dipika, Brhad-bhagavatamrta, Ujjvala-nilamani etc.) **may be cited as references** at TRD for the
spiritual world: Goloka and Vraja, the eternal associates, their rasas and services (trd-41 to trd-48, trd-33). Prabhupada's
books still give the principle and the main line. These books add the acharyas' descriptions, cited by book, chapter
and verse with the edition, in short quotes. Pages stay `sensitive: true` for the head's review.

## 3. Prayers and songs: any level, and rich for TRD

Stavavali and Stava-mala, Manah-siksa, Saranagati, Gitavali, Kalyana-kalpataru, Prarthana, Caitanya-candramrta,
Navadvipa-sataka, the astakas (Sad-gosvamy-astaka, Damodarastaka, Siksastaka, etc.). Used for the mood in trd-12,
trd-21 and trd-22, and in lower batches where simple.

## 4. Histories and lives: any level (DPS acharya lives already use them)

Caitanya-mangala, Caitanya-carita (Murari Gupta, Kavi Karnapura), Caitanya-candrodaya, Narottama-vilasa, Prema-vilasa,
Bhakti-ratnakara, Syamananda-prakasa, Karnananda, Abhirama Gosvami, Sri Krishna-vijaya, Bhaktivinoda's Autobiography.
Narratives only, with the usual care over editions.

**Texts fetched 29 Oct** (research/books/): Gopala-campu (Jiva Gosvami) and Radha-Krsna-ganoddesa-dipika (Rupa Gosvami)
are custom-font PDFs whose text layer is scrambled. They were decoded with `tools/decode_font_ocr.py` (map learned from
OCR'd pages) and `tools/decode_font_patterns.py` (map solved from word patterns). The English reads cleanly. In
Ganoddesa-dipika the Sanskrit before each verse is in a second font and stays garbled, so take the Sanskrit from another
source. Govinda-lilamrta 1–2 extracted normally. None of the files names a translator: confirm the edition before quoting.
