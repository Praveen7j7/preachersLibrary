# Courses and Gaudiya research: separate from the batches (head, 29 Oct 2026)

The library has four parts:
1. **Batches** (VL1, DYS, VL2, VL3, SPS, DPS, BSS, TRD): topic sessions, as now.
2. **Courses**: systematic deep study of whole books, below. They sit beside the batches, not inside any of them.
3. **Gaudiya Research**: the Research Hub (scholarly and empirical papers on Gaudiya / Vaisnava siddhanta by domain,
   each Crossref-verified, with "what it shows / limits"), also its own section.
4. **Important slokas** (head, 29 Oct): the key verses of every book the courses cover (BG, Sri Isopanisad, NOI,
   NOD/BRS verses, SB, CC), each with Devanagari, transliteration, translation and a short "what it means / when to use
   it", tagged by topic and level, with memorisation lists per batch and course and links to the pages that use it.
   Built by script from vedabase (no model tokens): the verses our pages cite, plus the courses' memorisation verses.
   The BBT owns the translations: fine for this internal, link-only site; ask the BBT before any public release.

On the website each part gets its own section and home-page cards. There is no "BBS" batch (head, 29 Oct).

## The four courses

Named after ISKCON's sastric degrees; the library prepares the **study sessions**. It does not run exams or award
the degrees (that is ISKCON's Ministry of Education). All texts are Srila Prabhupada's BBT editions on vedabase, so
nothing needs to be obtained.

| Course | Id | Books | Sessions (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bhakti-sastri** | `sastri-NN` | Bhagavad-gita As It Is (18 ch.), Sri Isopanisad, Nectar of Instruction, Nectar of Devotion | ~40: BG 20 (ch. 2 and 18 in two parts), ISO 4, NOI 4, NOD 12 |
| **Bhakti-vaibhava** | `vaibhava-NN` | Srimad-Bhagavatam Cantos 1–6 (138 ch.) | ~70: about two chapters a session (long chapters alone) |
| **Bhaktivedanta** | `vedanta-NN` | Srimad-Bhagavatam Cantos 7–12 (197 ch.) | ~95: about two chapters a session; Canto 10 by pastime groups |
| **Bhakti-sarvabhauma** | `sarvabhauma-NN` | Sri Caitanya-caritamrta, Adi, Madhya, Antya (62 ch.) | ~65: a chapter a session, the longest Madhya chapters in two |

From SB Canto 10 ch. 14 onward, pages say "BBT translation, completed by Srila Prabhupada's disciples".

**Session format:** a 2-hour study session (up to 3 hours, the library's limit), like SPS onward. Timing is the time
available, not a length to fill.

**Study-session page** (the topic template, adapted to a text):
- **Preacher:** where the chapter sits (context, previous and next); the chapter's flow by verse groups; the key
  verses, verified, with purport points; siddhanta and its links to other texts (Sandarbhas, BRS and others from
  `research/advanced-books.md` where they add depth); likely questions; H.1 how to start; opening and closing check
  questions with answers; discussion; "R. Adapting for another level".
- **Student booklet:** a summary someone who missed the session can follow, key verses, words to know, questions
  with answers behind "Show answers", a self-check, and what to read before the next session.

## Order (proposed)

Bhakti-sastri first (it underlies the others and the batches), then Bhakti-vaibhava, Bhaktivedanta and
Bhakti-sarvabhauma. Each course is written in batches of sessions like the topic batches, with the head's review.
