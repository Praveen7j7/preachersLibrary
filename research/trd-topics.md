# TRD (temple residents): proposed topic list, 29 Oct 2026

**Status: approved by the head, 29 Oct 2026** (groups G and H added the same day at his request). Not on the IYF
catalogue; ids `trd-NN` are the library's own.

**Who TRD is for (head, 29 Oct):** young men who have joined the brahmacari ashram, and the full-time preachers living
in the temple. They have already given their lives; TRD deepens that life. Its four aims:
1. **Missionary spirit:** contribute to Srila Prabhupada's mission and preach.
2. **Depth of connection with Krishna:** bhajan that grows, not routine.
3. **Non-duplicity:** sincerity with Krishna, guru, authorities, peers and oneself.
4. **Relishing transcendental sweetness and happiness** while doing all this.
5. **Advanced siddhanta:** the six Sandarbhas and the other advanced Gaudiya texts, which no lower batch takes (group F).

**Session format:** up to 3 hours (writer-brief "Session formats"); content covers every level, so a topic can also be
given to BSS or DPS (section R).

**Not repeated here:** BSS already covers brahmacarya itself (BIKC chapters, sense control, dealings with women,
falldown), and DPS covers the Vaisnava qualities, humility, service attitude, the stages of bhakti, NOI 2–4 and the
Gosvamis' lives. TRD topics build on those and link to them.

Main sources are Srila Prabhupada's books, lectures and letters (vedabase); every verse goes through
`tools/verify-refs.mjs` when the page is written. The verse numbers below are the starting points.
