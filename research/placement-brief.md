# Placing candidate topics into batches: the head's requirements (28 Oct 2026)

**Task:** decide which candidate topics belong in which batch.

**Candidates:**
- the IYF presentation catalogue (`research/iyf-materials/iyf-decks/catalogue.csv`, 117 decks, with text)
- VOICE courses (`research/voice/courses.md`)
- the proposed topics in `docs/stage2-content-architecture.md` §7.1

**Rules:**
1. **Batch conditions and motive come first, from IYF's own documents.** First authority: the KRA/KPI workbook the head
   shared (`research/iyf-materials/iyf-kra/*.csv`, one CSV per tab; key tabs: "Vision_ mission_KRAs _amp_ activity",
   "VISION_ MISSION", "IYF Courses" (VL2 end expectations and VL3 eligibility), "System _amp_ KRAs", "core activties of IYF").
   Then `research/iyf-materials/iyf-docs/`:
   Documentation of Vedic Level 1, VL2 syllabus (`Copy of vl2_updated.pdf`, `Syllabus_Vedic Level-2`), the OTP speaker and
   facilitator guidelines. Fill gaps from Stage 2 §2.1. Write each batch's conditions, motive and mood down before placing
   anything.
2. **A topic is placed only if it matches the batch's conditions, its motive, and its mood**, and adds something the
   batch does not already cover.
   - Moods: VL1 light, curious, relatable, no pressure · VL2 questioning, reasoned · VL3 practical, encouraging · SPS
     steadying, relational · DPS serious, committed · BSS renounced, introspective.
   - A good topic in the wrong mood stays out, or is suggested for the batch whose mood it fits.
3. **Not every topic has to be added.** Selective, not exhaustive. Anything not placed goes to a "not placed" list with
   one reason:
   - **duplicate of `<topic id>`**: linked as material for that topic;
   - **off-mission;**
   - **outdated or event-bound;**
   - **too narrow** (better as an example inside `<topic id>`).
4. **Existing catalogue topics are never moved or renamed.** New placements are marked *Proposed — Not Yet Part of the
   Existing IYFD Catalogue* and need the head's approval.
5. **BVS is left out** (decision). **TRD** waits for the head's contents.
