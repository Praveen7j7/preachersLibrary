## 0. What applies to every batch

**Vision.** "Krishna conscious happy devotee, Krishna conscious contributing devotee & more Krishna conscious happy
devotee" — target 5,000 by 2025 (Vision tab, rows 1–7). A K.C. devotee is defined by **faith**, and faith "is defined by
his consistency in attentively chanting the holy name of Krishna at least minimum prescribed rounds, reading & hearing
the prescribed lectures" (Vision tab, rows 10–11). Happy = "satisfied in all situation of his life, by seeing the hand of
Krishna" (Vision tab, row 5).

**Central KRAs that every batch feeds** (System tab, "KRA – KEY RESULT AREAS" block):
1. increase in the number of rounds chanted, "at least till 16 rounds";
2. increase in weekly class attendance;
3. personal meeting with the facilitator, at least every 15 days;
4. consistency in, or increase of, the **sadhana score**;
5. other sadhana items beyond the score;
6. engagement and responsibility: "no. of devotees serving consistently with responsibility";
7. participation in festivals, camps, in-class activities, harinaam;
8. total count of devotees chanting at least 4 rounds daily.
Weekly monitoring: attendance report, sadhana card sending report, calling responses (System tab, "How/Who", row 1).

**Weekly class ladder.** "weekly classes for different level of students — VL-2, VL-3, SPS-1, SPS-2, DPS, BSS, BVS, VL-1"
(Vision tab, activity list, row "weekly classes"). Camps: DYS, Sankalpa, Utkarsh, DPS and BSS camps every 3 months, SPS camp
every 6 months; **DPS interview every 3 months**; Japa Retreat; UMANG and Annual Yatra every 6 months (Core tab).

**Wishes that cut across batches** (Vision tab, lower block): "Faith on the parampara system, ISKCON, chanting — topic of
the classes — basics of parampara"; "higher topics for the new level of students also — satisfying their intellectual
need"; "Vaishnav etiquette"; "students should feel IMPORTANT / role in this movement"; "Games in classes for VL-2 and
VL-3"; a weekday workshop each month on "1. Acharya, 2. festival, 3. Practical life application – PDC".

**Mood labels** (VL1 light · VL2 questioning · VL3 practical · SPS steadying · DPS serious · BSS renounced) are the
head's, from the placement brief rule 2. They are not in IYF's documents, but each section below shows which IYF
sentences support them.
