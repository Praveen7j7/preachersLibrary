# Book distribution: the great drum

> **TRD · up to 180 min · review: drafted (30 Oct 2026).** Group A. Srila Prabhupada's statements are quoted only where
> verified on vedabase (letters, room conversation); the Preaching Encyclopedia's other quotations are listed in O to
> verify before use. **The head's ruling (30 Oct 2026):** the VOICE "Don'ts" on not naming ISKCON and not revealing one's
> identity apply only to college preaching (see K.5).

## A. Topic overview

| | |
|---|---|
| Batch / level | TRD (temple residents), group A |

## B. Why this topic belongs at this level

trd-04 gave the idea (the press as the greater drum); this page is the service itself. Residents distribute books as a
duty; the difference between a resident who lasts ten years at it and one who burns out in two is usually the mood and
the words he carries. Srila Prabhupada's letters give both.

| Level | Treatment |
|---|---|
| VL1 | Not covered |
| VL2 | Why devotees give books: "whoever gets a book is benefited" (letter, 6 May 1977), one sentence |
| VL3 | Taking one book to one friend as the student's first preaching step |
| SPS | SB 1.5.11 and Srila Prabhupada's letters; a first supervised hour at a book table |
| DPS | Builds on dps-10 (service attitude) and dps-26: distribution as the Vaisnava's compassion in action |
| BSS | The brahmacari's main service: the letters on books, the mood of prayer, simple dress and conduct |
| **TRD (here)** | The **whole service**: mood, method, honesty, Srila Prabhupada's words, stories, and lasting at it |

**Not done here:** why we preach (trd-01); the acarya's plan (trd-04); the care of people who take a book (trd-06).

## C. Central message

Srila Prabhupada said: "This book distribution is the most important task in our Society… this is my life and soul
according to the order of my Guru Maharaja" (room conversation, 31 December 1976), and "there is no more direct way to
preach than to distribute Krsna conscious books. Whoever gets a book is benefitted" (letter, 6 May 1977). A resident
distributes books out of compassion, depending on Krishna, with clean conduct and honest words, and does not stop.

## D. Learning outcomes

Participants should be able to:
1. Quote three verified statements of Srila Prabhupada on book distribution with their dates.
2. Explain from SB 1.5.11 and CC Antya 20.12 why a book is sankirtana.
3. Describe a simple, honest method for approaching a person with a book, and say what must never be done.
4. Apply NOI 3 (enthusiasm, patience, confidence) to the slow weeks.
5. Tell one verified story of a book's effect, as the source tells it.

## E. Scriptural foundation

**SB 1.5.11** — Verified (full treatment in trd-04). Transcendental literature brings "a revolution in the impious lives of this world's misdirected civilization"; the purport: "by broadcasting the holy name and fame of the Supreme Lord, the polluted atmosphere of the world will change."
- **Use:** in the talk; the book distributor's own verse.

**CC Antya 20.12** — Verified (trd-04). Victory to the chanting of the holy name; the book carries the chanting.
- **Use:** in the talk, as the prayer before going out: *param vijayate sri-krsna-sankirtanam* (the Encyclopedia's "Do's and Don'ts" ends with the same line).

**NOI 3** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/noi/3/
- **Teaching:** Enthusiasm, endeavour with confidence, patience, following the regulative principles, giving up the association of nondevotees, following the previous acaryas.
- **Purport point:** "One should not be impatient in discharging devotional service but should take instructions from the spiritual master and execute them with patience." The purport recalls that the movement had no response at first and continued.
- **Use:** in the talk, for the slow weeks and the long years.

**CC Madhya 7.128** — Verified (trd-02). Srila Prabhupada quoted it in his letter of 6 May 1977 directly before saying there is "no more direct way to preach than to distribute Krsna conscious books".
- **Use:** in the talk: the book is the "krsna-upadesa" handed over.

**BG 18.68** — Verified (trd-01). The one who explains the Gita to devotees is guaranteed pure devotion.
- **Use:** one line: the distributor's own gain.

**SB 11.5.32** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/5/32/ · *BBT translation, completed by Srila Prabhupada's disciples*
- **Teaching:** The verse Srila Prabhupada quoted at the end of the 6 May 1977 letter: in Kali-yuga intelligent people worship the Lord by sankirtana.
- **Use:** background; R.1.

## F. Philosophical explanation

1. **A book is sankirtana.** The holy name's glories in print are "a different creation" (SB 1.5.11); handing them over is chanting that continues in the reader's house. Srila Prabhupada: "Preaching means book distribution."
2. **Why "most important".** His guru's order (trd-04) and his own history: "I never tried in India to construct big temples… But I was selling personally books." The resident who distributes is doing what the founder did himself.
3. **Everyone gains** (letter, 6 May 1977). The one who gets, reads, passes on, merely holds, or donates; and the distributor. This removes the fear of "wasting" a book on someone who may not read it.
4. **The mood.** Compassion (trd-01: Prahlada's love for those planning without Krishna), dependence ("Pray intensely", as the Encyclopedia's tips say; Markine verse 12 in trd-03: "I can simply repeat Your message"), and the six principles of NOI 3 for the long run.
5. **Honesty is part of the service.** Books are given as what they are. A book obtained by a trick teaches the reader to distrust the next devotee. Non-duplicity (TRD's third aim) applies at the book table first. Only in college preaching do the VOICE "Don'ts" apply (K.5).
6. **The distributor's own bhakti.** BG 18.68: pure devotion is "guaranteed" for the one who gives the message. Residents who fear that years on the street dry them up should read this with the letters: "Whenever I get report of my book selling I feel strength" (Encyclopedia, letter to Satsvarupa 8 Sep 1974; verify).
7. **Misunderstandings.** (a) "Numbers are the point": the point is the person and the book; numbers are a report of it. (b) "Any method that moves books is fine": the Encyclopedia itself says "Do not force them"; honesty and cleanliness are part of the method. (c) "I am not a distributor": every resident can carry two books and the mood.

## H. Opening and engagement

**H.1 How to start (first 5 minutes).** Put one BBT book on a table in front of the room. Say: "Eleven months before he
left this world, Srila Prabhupada said in Bombay that this is the most important task in our Society and 'my life and
soul'. Tonight we hear his words on it, learn the mood and the method, and practise." Read the 31 December 1976 passage.

**H.2 Opening questions**
1. "Who here first met Krishna consciousness through a book? What was it, and who gave it?"
2. "What is the hardest part of distributing a book: the first word, the refusal, or the slow afternoon?"
3. "Srila Prabhupada said he 'never tried' to build big temples but sold books personally. Does that surprise you?"

## I. Core teaching points

1. **The book is the drum (SB 1.5.11; CC Antya 20.12).** Literature about the Lord changes the atmosphere; victory belongs to the chanting it carries. Example: a book left on a bus shelf is still chanting next year. Transition: "Hear what he called it."
2. **"My life and soul" (31 Dec 1976).** The most important task, by his guru's order, done by him personally. Example: residents who distribute follow the founder's own daily practice. Transition: "He wrote this for ten years."
3. **The letters (1967–1977).** From "if I live or die… take very serious care for my publications" to "Whoever gets a book is benefitted." Example: the Ramesvara letter answers the resident who wants to "diversify" away from books: "do not divert your mind for a moment from this." Transition: "What mood carries this for years?"
4. **Mood (NOI 3; trd-03).** Enthusiasm, confidence, patience; prayer before going out; repeating Krishna's message, not one's own. Example: a slow afternoon is NOI 3's patience in practice. Transition: "And the method must match the mood."
5. **Method and honesty.** Clean dress, a smile, a clear voice, no force, no arguments, invite rather than push (Encyclopedia do's). Say who you are if asked; never trick a person into a book. Example: "I am a monk from the Hare Krishna temple; this is the Bhagavad-gita; may I show you one page?" Transition to J.

## J. Stories and illustrations

**Srila Prabhupada's own history (31 Dec 1976, Verified).** "I never tried in India to construct big temples, or even
in your country we didn't. I never tried. But I was selling personally books. That is the history. Sometimes they are
criticizing, 'What kind of sannyasi? He is doing book business.'" Lesson: the criticism was his too; he carried on.

**The Greatest Gita (Preaching Encyclopedia, "Amazing Stories"; a VOICE devotee's account).** Distributing in the coal
mines, devotees met an officer who had guided six Ph.D. students in Hindi literature and wrote for the Gita Press. He
said, "I have read eight commentaries on Bhagwad Gita. What new thing you have?" Pressed to look, he agreed to read only
the commentary on the first verse. After a few moments of Srila Prabhupada's purport to 1.1 he said, "This Bhagwad Gita
is really great. Its marvelous. Give me the entire set." Lesson: the book speaks when we only open it; the purport is
the preacher.

**The man on the Dadar train (Encyclopedia, "Special Devotee", Mukund Mala's account).** A drunk man on a Mumbai local
asked loudly for a Hindi book worth five hundred rupees; the first distributor ignored him. His partner Rajesh went to
him and explained the book. The man said he was too sinful to touch it; Rajesh told him to take it and chant Hare
Krishna; the man chanted and asked for it to be put in a bag. Lesson: the 6 May 1977 letter's "whoever gets a book is
benefitted" includes the person we are tempted to skip.

## K. Anticipated questions and objections

1. **"Isn't it just selling?"** Srila Prabhupada heard that criticism ("What kind of sannyasi?") and answered with his guru's order and his own practice. The donation funds the next printing; the book is the sankirtana.
2. **"Many books are never read."** The 1977 letter covers every case: holding, seeing, passing on, donating; "whoever gets a book is benefitted."
3. **"I feel dry after months on the street."** NOI 3: patience and confidence; BG 18.68: pure devotion is guaranteed for the one who gives the message; and read one of the letters each morning. If dryness lasts, speak to your authority (trd-09 treats this).
4. **"Should I argue when someone attacks the philosophy?"** The Encyclopedia says don't; invite him to a session instead. The book table is for giving, not winning.
5. **"The Encyclopedia says not to mention ISKCON or reveal our identity on campus. Is that right?"** The VOICE material lists this among its "Don'ts" ("Do not speak about ISKCON or VOICE… Only introduce as a voluntary youth club"; "Don't reveal your complete identity"). **The head's ruling (30 Oct 2026):** this applies only to college preaching. On a college campus, introduce the programme as a youth club and do not lead with ISKCON or VOICE, as the VOICE material says. In all other preaching, including book distribution, residents say who they are.
6. **"The tips quote percentages about body language (7%, 38%, 55%) and a suicide statistic. Can I use them?"** No. They are unsourced in the Encyclopedia and are left out of this page.

## L. Case studies

**Case 1: The quota (Invented case for discussion).** Before a festival marathon a leader sets each resident a daily
number. Suresh, two years in, begins pressing hesitant people and stops saying he is from the temple because "it slows
things down".
- **Dilemma:** More books, by a method the founder would not sign.
- **Principles:** 6 May 1977 letter (everyone benefits, including the distributor); Encyclopedia do's ("Do not force"); non-duplicity (TRD aim 3).
- **Questions:** Which of Suresh's changes serve the person in front of him? What does a book taken under pressure teach the reader about devotees? What should the leader change?
- **Takeaway:** Keep the marathon; drop the pressure and the concealment. Report numbers; serve people.

**Case 2: The one book a day (Invented case for discussion).** Hari, in the kitchen all day, decides to carry two books
and offer one to a vendor or delivery man each day. After a month he has given twenty books and started three
conversations.
- **Principles:** CC Madhya 7.128 ("whomever you meet"); 11 Oct 1974 letter ("do not divert your mind for a moment").
- **Takeaway:** Every resident can be a distributor at his own scale; the drum does not need a table.

## M. Discussion and reflection

**M.1 Closing check questions**
1. What did Srila Prabhupada call book distribution on 31 December 1976? (The most important task in our Society; "my life and soul according to the order of my Guru Maharaja".)
2. According to the 6 May 1977 letter, who is benefited by a book? (Whoever gets it, reads it, passes it on, holds it, donates for it; and the distributor.)
3. What does SB 1.5.11 say such literature brings about? (A revolution in the impious lives of the world's misdirected civilisation.)
4. Which three NOI 3 principles carry a distributor through slow weeks? (Enthusiasm, confidence, patience.)
5. Name two things the Encyclopedia's do's and don'ts forbid. (Forcing a purchase; arguing; speaking ill of others; and so on.)
6. What did he write to Ramesvara in 1974 about dividing one's attention? ("Do not divert your mind for a moment from this.")

**M.2 Activity (35 min): approaches.** In threes (distributor, person, observer), two-minute approaches: a student at
a bus stop; a shopkeeper; a parent at a school gate. The distributor must: say who he is in one sentence, show one page,
say one sentence of what the book gives, and accept a "no" gracefully. The observer checks for force, argument or
concealment and gives one line of feedback. Rotate three times.

**M.3 Reflection (private).** "Which of Srila Prabhupada's sentences on books will I keep in my pocket this month, and why that one?"

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary

In Bombay on 31 December 1976, eleven months before he left this world, Srila Prabhupada said: "This book distribution
is the most important task in our Society… Because this is my life and soul according to the order of my Guru
Maharaja… I never tried in India to construct big temples, or even in your country we didn't. I never tried. But I was
selling personally books. That is the history." In the same conversation he said, "Preaching means book distribution."

The scripture behind this is SB 1.5.11: literature describing the Lord is "a different creation" meant "to bring about a
revolution in the impious lives of this world's misdirected civilization"; by spreading it, Srila Prabhupada writes,
"the polluted atmosphere of the world will change." A book is the chanting of the holy name in print, and Lord Caitanya
declared victory for that chanting (CC Antya 20.12).

Srila Prabhupada wrote about books for ten years. In 1967, ill in New York: "if I live or die you should take very
serious care for my publications." In 1974: "Whatever progress we have made, it is simply to distributing these books. So
go on, and do not divert your mind for a moment from this." In May 1977: "there is no more direct way to preach than to
distribute Krsna conscious books. Whoever gets a book is benefitted. If he reads the book he is benefitted still more, or
if he gives the book to someone else… both… Even if one does not read the book but simply holds it and sees it, he is
benefitted… And anyone who distributes these transcendental literatures, he is also benefitted."

The mood is compassion for the person and dependence on Krishna, with the patience and confidence of NOI 3 for slow
weeks. The method is clean and honest: neat dress, a smile, one page shown, one sentence of what the book gives, no
force, no argument, and saying plainly who we are when asked. A book obtained by a trick is not the drum.

## S2. Key ideas

- Book distribution was Srila Prabhupada's "life and soul", by his guru's order, and his own daily practice.
- A book is sankirtana in print (SB 1.5.11; CC Antya 20.12).
- Everyone a book touches is benefited, and so is the distributor (letter, 6 May 1977).
- Patience, confidence and enthusiasm (NOI 3) carry the service through slow weeks.
- Honesty and no force are part of the method, not optional extras.

## S3. Verses to remember

- **SB 1.5.11:** transcendental literature is "directed toward bringing about a revolution in the impious lives of this world's misdirected civilization." *For me:* the book in my hand is that revolution.
- **CC Antya 20.12:** "Let there be all victory for the chanting of the holy name of Lord Krsna." *For me:* my prayer before I go out.
- **NOI 3:** enthusiasm, confidence, patience. *For me:* the slow afternoon is where patience is practised.

## S4. Words to know

- **Sankirtana:** congregational chanting of the holy name; here, also spreading it in print.
- **Brhad-mrdanga:** the "greater drum", the press and its books (trd-04).
- **BBT:** the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust, which prints Srila Prabhupada's books.
- **Marathon:** a period of intensified book distribution, usually before a festival.
- **Param vijayate sri-krsna-sankirtanam:** "all victory to the chanting of Krishna's holy name" (CC Antya 20.12).

## S5. Questions and answers

1. **Isn't this just selling books?** Srila Prabhupada heard that criticism and answered with his guru's order and his own practice; the donation prints the next book, the book is the chanting.
2. **What if the person never reads it?** The 1977 letter says even holding and seeing it benefits him, and passing it on benefits two.
3. **I get discouraged after refusals.** NOI 3 asks for patience and confidence; the founder's movement "had no response at first" and continued. Read one of his letters each morning.
4. **Should I debate someone who attacks the philosophy?** No; invite him to a session. The book table is for giving.
5. **Should I hide that I am from the temple?** Say who you are when asked and never deny it. (The temple's exact standard for campus introductions is with the head.)

## S6. Check your understanding

1. What did Srila Prabhupada call book distribution on 31 December 1976?
2. According to SB 1.5.11, what is transcendental literature meant to bring about?
3. List four kinds of people the 6 May 1977 letter says are benefited by a book.
4. Which NOI 3 principles help on a slow day?
5. True or false: Srila Prabhupada said he built big temples in India before selling books.
6. Name two things a distributor must never do.

**Answers:** 1. The most important task in our Society; "my life and soul". 2. A revolution in the impious lives of the
world's misdirected civilisation. 3. Any four of: the one who gets it, reads it, passes it on, holds and sees it, donates;
and the distributor. 4. Enthusiasm, confidence, patience. 5. False: "I never tried… But I was selling personally books."
6. Force a person; argue; conceal who he is; speak ill of others (any two).
