## B. Why this topic belongs at this level
A brahmacari lives inside a culture that tells him the opposite of what he has vowed. The earlier pages gave the drive (bss-21) and the consequences (bss-22). This page covers the environment: why the pressure seems everywhere, what the scriptures say of the age, and how to guard the mind in a connected world, with chanting and the association of devotees as the chief shelter.

## C. Central message
The age of Kali places stimulation everywhere, so a devotee does not rely on a strong will alone; he surrounds the mind with the holy name, hearing, devotees and service, so that a higher taste replaces the lower one.

## D. Learning outcomes
1. Describe two Bhagavatam statements about the nature of the age (SB 12.2.1, 12.2.3).
2. Explain how contemplating an object leads to desire (BG 2.62) and how to interrupt the chain.
3. Name the ladder senses, mind, intelligence, soul (BG 3.42) and say what BG 3.43 asks us to do.
4. Build a one-page daily plan that includes digital habits, chanting and association.
5. Speak of the surrounding society with compassion rather than contempt.

## E. Scriptural foundation
Quotations are from the BBT translations in `research/verification/bss-23.json`. Canto 12 (and Canto 11) translations are BBT, completed by Srila Prabhupada's disciples. Purport points were written after reading each full purport on vedabase.io; the extracts quoted are saved under `purportExtracts` in the same file. The purports of SB 12.2.1 and 12.2.3 (Canto 12) were written by Srila Prabhupada's disciples, not by Srila Prabhupada himself; say so if you quote them.

**BG 2.62** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/62/
> "While contemplating the objects of the senses, a person develops attachment for them, and from such attachment lust develops, and from lust anger arises."
- Context: Krsna describes how a person falls when the mind is not fixed on Him (the next verse, BG 2.63, is on bss-22).
- **Purport point:** "The senses require real engagements, and if they are not engaged in the transcendental loving service of the Lord, they will certainly seek engagement in the service of materialism." "a sincere devotee of the Lord shuns all material sense enjoyment due to his higher taste for spiritual enjoyment in the association of the Lord. That is the secret of success." And: "One who is not… in Kṛṣṇa consciousness, however powerful he may be in controlling the senses by artificial repression, is sure ultimately to fail." The purport's example is Haridasa Thakura, who as a young devotee "was similarly allured by the incarnation of Māyā-devī, but Haridāsa easily passed the test because of his unalloyed devotion to Lord Kṛṣṇa" (J).
- Application (preacher's): the first link is contemplation, which is where media and screens do their work; this is why where the eyes and mind linger matters.
- Use: in the talk.

**BG 2.64** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/64/
> "But a person free from all attachment and aversion and able to control his senses through regulative principles of freedom can obtain the complete mercy of the Lord."
- **Purport point:** "one may externally control the senses by some artificial process, but unless the senses are engaged in the transcendental service of the Lord, there is every chance of a fall." "The Kṛṣṇa conscious person is concerned only with the satisfaction of Kṛṣṇa"; the freedom of the verse is "the causeless mercy of the Lord."
- Use: in the talk; the goal is the Lord's mercy.

**BG 2.59** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/59/
> "…ceasing such engagements by experiencing a higher taste, he is fixed in consciousness."
- **Purport point:** restriction is "something like restricting a diseased person from certain types of eatables. The patient, however, neither likes such restrictions nor loses his taste"; but "one who has tasted the beauty of the Supreme Lord Kṛṣṇa… no longer has a taste for dead, material things." Restrictions are "only good until one actually has a taste for Kṛṣṇa consciousness."
- Use: in the talk; the higher taste is chanting and service.

**BG 3.42** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/42/
> "The working senses are superior to dull matter; mind is higher than the senses; intelligence is still higher than the mind; and he [the soul] is even higher than the intelligence."
- **Purport point:** "directly understanding the constitutional position of the soul is the solution of the whole problem. With intelligence one has to seek out the constitutional position of the soul and then engage the mind always in Kṛṣṇa consciousness." "A neophyte spiritualist is generally advised to keep aloof from the objects of the senses", but a mind engaged in Krsna consciousness makes the senses "no more effective than serpents with broken fangs." Yet "unless it is strengthened by association with Kṛṣṇa in Kṛṣṇa consciousness, there is every chance of falling down due to the agitated mind."
- Use: in the talk; the ladder.

**BG 3.43** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/43/
> "…one should steady the mind by deliberate spiritual intelligence [Kṛṣṇa consciousness] and thus – by spiritual strength – conquer this insatiable enemy known as lust."
- Teaching: the weapon is Krsna consciousness, not mental effort alone.
- **Purport point:** "by the strength of Kṛṣṇa consciousness, one can control the material senses, the mind and the intelligence"; this is done "by gradually developing Kṛṣṇa consciousness", and "artificial attempts to control the senses by the so-called practice of yogic postures can never help a man toward spiritual life. He must be trained in Kṛṣṇa consciousness by higher intelligence."

**BG 6.26** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/6/26/
> "From wherever the mind wanders due to its flickering and unsteady nature, one must certainly withdraw it and bring it back under the control of the Self."
- **Purport point:** "One who controls the mind… is called gosvāmī… and one who is controlled by the mind is called go-dāsa, or the servant of the senses." The method: "the senses are engaged in the service of Hṛṣīkeśa… Serving Kṛṣṇa with purified senses is called Kṛṣṇa consciousness. That is the way of bringing the senses under full control." Bringing the mind back means bringing it back to Krsna's service.
- Use: in the talk; the habit of bringing the mind back.

**SB 1.17.38–39** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/17/38/ and /39/
> "…gambling, drinking, prostitution and animal slaughter" (38); "…gold… falsity, intoxication, lust, envy and enmity" (39).
- Context: King Pariksit allots Kali places of residence.
- **Purport point:** these are the principles of irreligion ("pride, prostitution, intoxication and falsehood") that "counteract the four principles of religion, namely austerity, cleanliness, mercy and truthfulness"; it is the duty of leaders "to see that the principles of religion… are established." The purport on 38 also notes that the Vedic regulations on such matters are "meant for a particular class of men, and not for all" and are "never to be encouraged" as an aim. The purport on 39 explains that Kali is allowed where there is gold "because wherever there is gold there are all the above-mentioned four things, and over and above them there is enmity also."
- Use: in the talk; Kali is given places that have these qualities.

**SB 12.2.1** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/12/2/1/
> "…religion, truthfulness, cleanliness, tolerance, mercy, duration of life, physical strength and memory will all diminish day by day because of the powerful influence of the Age of Kali."
- **Purport point** (by Srila Prabhupada's disciples): "Satyam, truthfulness, is also diminishing, simply because people do not know what the truth is"; tolerance is diminishing "because there is no practical method by which people can purify themselves and thus become free of envy. Unless one is purified by chanting the holy names of the Lord in an authorized program of spiritual improvement, the mind will be overwhelmed by anger, envy and all sorts of small-mindedness."

**SB 12.2.3** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/12/2/3/
> "Men and women will live together merely because of superficial attraction, and success in business will depend on deceit. Womanliness and manliness will be judged according to one’s expertise in sex, and a man will be known as a brāhmaṇa just by his wearing a thread."
- **Purport point** (by Srila Prabhupada's disciples): "fundamental human institutions such as marriage and child-rearing should also be dedicated to that great objective" of spiritual liberation; the sexual impulse is "based on the unnatural identification of the self with the body", and the Vedic scriptures prescribe "sacred marriage" so that the family man "can derive adequate satisfaction for his senses and simultaneously please the Lord within his heart… The Lord then purifies him of material desire." The purport continues: "In Kali-yuga this deep understanding has been almost lost, and, as stated in this verse, men and women combine like animals, solely on the basis of mutual attraction to bodies made of flesh, bone, membrane, blood and so on."
- Use: in the talk, read as it is.

**SB 12.3.51** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/12/3/51/
> "…although Kali-yuga is an ocean of faults, there is still one good quality about this age: Simply by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra, one can become free from material bondage…"
- **Purport point** (by Srila Prabhupada's disciples): "Just as one powerful king can kill innumerable thieves, one brilliant spiritual quality can destroy all the contamination of this age. It is impossible to overestimate the importance of chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa… especially in this fallen age."
- Use: in the talk; the answer to the age.

**SB 5.18.12** · Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/5/18/12/
> "All the demigods and their exalted qualities, such as religion, knowledge and renunciation, become manifest in the body of one who has developed unalloyed devotion…"
- **Purport point:** "All living entities are part and parcel of Kṛṣṇa, and therefore when they revive their original Kṛṣṇa consciousness, they possess all the good qualities of Kṛṣṇa in a small quantity. When one engages himself in the nine processes of devotional service… one's heart becomes purified." Good character is a result of hearing, chanting and service, not a separate project.
- Use: in the talk; the positive side: good qualities arrive with devotion.

## F. Philosophical explanation
**F.1 The environment.** BIKC chapter 7 says media and advertising keep the public in a state of constant stimulation and that promises of fulfilment leave people empty. Present this as the author's analysis. For global audiences, avoid statistics unless you can cite them; this page cites none.

**F.2 The Gita's chain.** BG 2.62 names contemplation as the start; BIKC's recommendation follows: guard what you contemplate. The Krsna conscious method is that the mind must be given something better to contemplate: the holy name, the Lord's pastimes, devotees' talk (SB 12.3.51; BG 2.59). The BG 2.62 purport gives the reason: the senses "require real engagements"; unless engaged in the Lord's service they "will certainly seek engagement in the service of materialism."

**F.3 The ladder.** BG 3.42: senses, mind, intelligence, then the soul. A rule that works at the sense level (filters, distance) helps; but for a lasting result the intelligence must be engaged by Krsna consciousness (BG 3.43).

**F.4 The digital routine as support.** Practical aids are only supports: phone kept out of the sleeping room; no private scrolling in the late hours; filters installed by agreement with a senior; devices used for hearing lectures and service. They help the mind because the devotee is also chanting with attention, hearing the Bhagavatam and associating with devotees, which give the higher taste.

**F.5 Compassion.** The Vaisnava is sorry for those caught in this (BIKC ch. 7, summarised). He does not mock them. Many people are lonely and seek love; the Krsna conscious answer for them is the same holy name.

**F.6 Misunderstandings.** (1) "All media is sinful": no; the issue is what we contemplate. (2) "Willpower is enough": BG 3.43 says Krsna consciousness is the strength. (3) "If I fail once, the vow is over": see bss-40 (falldown); recovery is real.

## H. Opening and engagement
**H.1 How to start.** (1) "Today is about the environment we live in; we do not judge anyone's past." (2) Show a blank page titled "My day" and tell them they will fill it. (3) Read SB 12.3.51 first: begin with the hopeful verse. (4) Ask H.2.

**H.2 Opening questions.**
1. How many times today has a screen asked for your attention? (Easy; a number is enough.)
2. What do you feel in your mind after an hour with a phone: fuller or emptier?
3. What helps you feel truly satisfied after a day: a meal, a service, a kirtan? (Bring answers toward the holy name.)

## I. Core teaching points
1. **The age has a character.** SB 12.2.1, 12.2.3, SB 1.17.38–39. Transition: "Do we blame the age or find shelter in it?"
2. **Where the chain starts.** BG 2.62. Application (preacher's): the first moment, contemplation, is the easiest to interrupt.
3. **The ladder to climb.** BG 3.42–43.
4. **The remedy of the age.** SB 12.3.51, SB 11.5.32 (mentioned only). Chanting is the primary; it gives the higher taste of BG 2.59.
5. **Practical guarding, in support.** the digital routine of F.4, never as a substitute for chanting, hearing and association.

## J. Stories and illustrations
- **Pariksit and Kali** (SB 1.17): Kali is a person in the Bhagavatam; he is allotted places by the king. **Devotional lesson:** even in a faulty age the Lord's chosen king acted by dharma, and the same age comes with the shelter of the holy name (SB 12.3.51). Full detail is not needed here.
- **Haridasa Thakura** (named in the BG 2.62 purport): as a young devotee he was allured by an incarnation of Maya-devi and "easily passed the test because of his unalloyed devotion to Lord Kṛṣṇa." Tell only what the purport says; if you wish to tell the full history, read it first in Caitanya-caritamrta (not summarised here). **Devotional lesson:** protection is love for the Lord's name, the higher taste, not mere strength.
- Everyday image (invented, label it): a crowded railway platform. A person can stand in it and keep his eyes on the one friend he is meeting; ends in the Krsna conscious point: the devotee keeps his mind on Krsna's name and service wherever he stands.

## K. Anticipated questions and objections
- *Is this blaming women or the whole society?* No: it describes the age's culture. Many people there suffer; we wish them well (F.5).
- *Is it unrealistic to avoid screens?* Use them for hearing and service; guard private time; agree on filters with a senior.
- *I live in a hostel or work in an office, I cannot escape.* Keep what is in your control: your hours of chanting, your company at meals, your phone at night.
- *Does a filter solve it?* No: it supports. The higher taste comes from the name.
- *I struggle with compulsive habits.* Please speak to a trusted senior; a qualified counsellor can add support. Tele-MANAS 14416 (India) or your local service.

## L. Case studies
**L.1 Invented case for discussion.** *Situation:* Ravi works a night shift at a call centre and returns at dawn to the ashram; his phone is his only free time. *Dilemma:* long idle hours, a pull towards images. *Principles:* BG 2.62, BG 2.59, SB 12.3.51. *Questions:* (a) At which link of BG 2.62 can he interrupt? (b) Which Krsna conscious engagement could fill the idle hours? (c) Who could he ask for help? *Analysis:* attach a fixed block of attentive chanting and hearing to the end of the shift, ask a senior to set filters, find a devotee friend for evening kirtan. *Takeaway:* interrupt early; replace with the name.

**M.2 Activities.** (a) *My day plan (15 min):* each person fills a one-page day with chanting rounds, hearing, a service, a devotee's company, and phone rules; share one line in pairs. # Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary
Today we studied the environment in which a brahmacari lives. The Bhagavatam describes the age of Kali as an age in which religion, truthfulness, mercy and memory diminish (SB 12.2.1) and in which relationships are often built on superficial attraction (SB 12.2.3). The mind is caught step by step: contemplation of objects, attachment, lust, anger (BG 2.62). So the first link, what we keep looking at and thinking about, is the best place to act.

Krsna gives the ladder: senses, mind, intelligence, then the soul, and asks us to steady the mind with Krsna consciousness (BG 3.42–43). The age also has a gift: by chanting the Hare Krsna mantra one can be free from bondage (SB 12.3.51), and a higher taste lets the lower fade (BG 2.59).

Practical supports help: a day plan, phone out of the bedroom, filters agreed with a senior, using devices for hearing and service. These support chanting, hearing and the company of devotees; they do not replace them. If a habit feels compulsive, talk to a senior and consider a qualified counsellor.

## S2. Key ideas
- Where the mind lingers, desire follows (BG 2.62).
- The soul is above the mind; Krsna consciousness steadies it (BG 3.43).
- The holy name is the gift of the age (SB 12.3.51).
- A plan for the day protects us; help is available.

## S3. Verses to remember
- **BG 2.62** · contemplation leads to attachment, lust, anger · guard the first link.
- **BG 3.43** · steady the mind by Krsna consciousness · my strength is Krsna's.
- **SB 12.3.51** · by chanting Hare Krsna one is free from bondage · my first shelter.

## S4. Words to know
- **Kali-yuga:** the present age of quarrel.
- **Contemplation:** repeated thinking about something.
- **Intelligence:** the faculty that decides.
- **Mahamantra:** Hare Krsna Hare Krsna Krsna Krsna Hare Hare / Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare.

## S5. Questions and answers
1. *Is all media bad?* No: what matters is what we contemplate; use it for hearing and service.
2. *Is filtering enough?* No: it supports; the higher taste comes from the name.
3. *Why is it hard in this age?* The age gives constant stimulation (SB 12.2.1).
4. *What if I slip?* See a senior; do not give up; later pages explain recovery.
5. *Where do I find help?* A trusted senior; a counsellor for compulsive patterns.

## S6. Check your understanding

2. What is above the mind in BG 3.42?
3. What is the good quality of Kali-yuga (SB 12.3.51)?
4. Name two supports for guarding the mind.

**Answers:** 1. Contemplating objects of the senses. 2. Intelligence. 3. Chanting the Hare Krsna mantra frees one from bondage. 4. Examples: phone out of the bedroom; filters with a senior; attentive chanting; devotees' company. 5. False.
