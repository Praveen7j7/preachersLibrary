# Addressing various misconceptions about ISKCON

> **VL3 · 180 min · review: senior-review · SENSITIVE (controversy): the head reviews this page before use.** IYF has no
> notes or deck for this topic; the site lists four lectures by HG Sundar Gopal Prabhu (sources-map; "hear next" in
> O). Stage 2 §2.5 applies: official positions are given *with their published source*, and where IYF has not yet
> confirmed which documents to cite (Stage 1, decision 6) the item is marked **Requires source review**. The page
> treats ten misconceptions a Delhi college student actually meets. It is fair to the people who hold them: most are
> parents, professors and friends who have never been told otherwise. Nothing here compares ISKCON favourably with
> other faiths or Vaisnava lines, and nothing takes a political side.

## B. Why this topic belongs at this level

Builds on vl2-07 (why ISKCON's guidance is reliable) and vl3-08 (where to take a doubt); it supplies the content that vl3-13 (dealing with relatives) assumes.

IYF's VL3 motive includes being "well known in challenges from society, friends and relatives and appropriate dealing
with them" (KRA tab; `research/batch-criteria.md` §3). At VL2 the student was convinced *for himself* (VL2-07:
"Identifying the divine guide"); at VL3 he must speak *for the movement* to people who love him and worry about him.
This page prepares that speech. VL3-13 (parents, relatives and friends) teaches *how* to deal with the people; this
page supplies *what* to say about ISKCON.

**Not done here:**
- Comparing ISKCON with other religions or other Vaisnava institutions. Every answer is about what ISKCON is, not what
  others are not.
- Internal controversies (ritvik, jiva-fall, etc.): sps-19 / dps-43.
- Political questions. F.8 says only that ISKCON is not a political body and that Krsna consciousness is for everyone.
- Detailed history of past institutional failures. F.10 gives the honest frame and marks the sources as requiring the
  head's confirmation.
- Madhurya-rasa. F.9 gives Sukadeva Gosvami's own answer to Pariksit's question and stops; sps-33 takes it further.

## C. Central message

ISKCON is the modern form of an ancient disciplic succession teaching *sanatana-dharma*, the eternal function of every
soul (BG 4.2; SB 1.2.6), brought to the world by an Indian acharya in fulfilment of Sri Caitanya's wish (CC Adi 9.41).
Most objections to it dissolve when the facts are stated plainly, kindly and with their sources; the few that do not are
honest questions to be carried to seniors, not argued on the spot.

## D. Learning outcomes

By the end, participants should be able to:
1. State in two sentences where ISKCON comes from (the Brahma-Madhva-Gaudiya parampara; Srila Prabhupada; 1966) and
   what it teaches (sanatana-dharma; BG 4.2; SB 1.2.6).
2. Answer "why only Krsna?" with BG 9.23 and SB 4.31.14 in a way that respects, and does not mock, the worship of
   other deities.
3. Answer the money, "brainwashing" and "foreign" objections with BG 9.27, BG 3.8, CC Adi 9.41 and SB 2.4.18.
4. Explain public kirtana from CC Adi 17.21.
5. Recognise the questions that must be referred (past controversies; the gopis; theological disputes) and know what
   an honest holding answer sounds like.

## E. Scriptural foundation

**Use:** BG 4.2, 9.23, SB 4.31.14, CC Adi 9.41 and CC Adi 17.21 carry the talk; SB 10.33.26–30 (deeper verse; the talk uses only its plain meaning) only for K.9; the
rest supports particular answers.

**BG 4.2** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/2/
> "This supreme science was thus received through the chain of disciplic succession, and the saintly kings understood it in that way. But in course of time the succession was broken, and therefore the science as it is appears to be lost."
- **Context:** Krsna tells Arjuna the Gita was spoken to the sun-god and passed down (4.1); the chain broke; He speaks
  it again.
- **Teaching:** Spiritual knowledge is received through succession, and successions can break and be re-established.
- **Purport point:** "there are so many editions of the Gītā (especially in English), but almost all of them are not
  according to authorized disciplic succession"; hence "an edition of the Gītā in English, as it is received by the
  paramparā".
- **Application (preacher's):** The Introduction to *Bhagavad-gita As It Is* prints the succession: Krsna, Brahma,
  Narada, Vyasa, Madhva… Madhavendra Puri, Isvara Puri, Lord Caitanya, Rupa… Bhaktivinoda, Gaurakisora,
  Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada (32 names). ISKCON (1966) is a branch of a tree whose
  roots are in the Gita itself; "new" describes the society, not the teaching.
- **Use:** in the talk; the first answer.

**BG 4.34** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/34/
> "Just try to learn the truth by approaching a spiritual master. Inquire from him submissively and render service unto him…"
- **Purport point:** "No one can be a bona fide spiritual master without following this principle of disciplic
  succession."
- **Use:** background for K.12 (why devotees honour Srila Prabhupada).

**BG 9.23** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/9/23/
> "Those who are devotees of other gods and who worship them with faith actually worship only Me, O son of Kuntī, but they do so in a wrong way."
- **Purport point:** The demigods "are, so to speak, different officers and directors in the government of the
  Supreme Lord"; watering leaves without watering the root is done "without sufficient knowledge"; "Kṛṣṇa does not
  approve the unnecessary worship of the demigods."
- **Application (preacher's):** The verse says such worshippers "actually worship only Me": their faith is real and
  reaches Krsna; the method is incomplete. Teach it in that order: first the respect, then the correction. Never
  "they worship false gods".
- **Use:** in the talk.

**SB 4.31.14** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/4/31/14/
> "As pouring water on the root of a tree energizes the trunk, branches, twigs and everything else, and as supplying food to the stomach enlivens the senses and limbs of the body, simply worshiping the Supreme Personality of Godhead through devotional service automatically satisfies the demigods, who are parts of that Supreme Personality."
- **Purport point:** "Sometimes people ask why this Kṛṣṇa consciousness movement simply advocates worship of Kṛṣṇa to
  the exclusion of the demigods. The answer is given in this verse." Serving Krsna "means automatically serving all the
  demigods."
- **Application (preacher's):** Srila Prabhupada himself frames this verse as the answer to our exact question. The
  devotee does not disrespect Siva or Durga; he serves the root and thereby serves them.
- **Use:** in the talk; the second answer.

**BG 9.27** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/9/27/
> "Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer or give away, and whatever austerities you perform – do that, O son of Kuntī, as an offering to Me."
- **Purport point:** "all surplus money accumulated should be utilized in furthering the Kṛṣṇa consciousness
  movement."
- **Application (preacher's):** The money answer (F.4): money is not avoided; it is engaged. The test is what it is
  used for.
- **Use:** in the talk.

**NOI 2** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/noi/2/
> "One's devotional service is spoiled when he becomes too entangled in the following six activities: (1) eating more than necessary or collecting more funds than required…"
- **Application (preacher's):** The standard by which devotees themselves judge money: *atyāhāra*, collecting more than
  required, spoils devotion. The movement's own rule, cited against the movement's critics' fear.
- **Use:** in the talk, one line.

**BG 9.32** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/9/32/
> "O son of Pṛthā, those who take shelter in Me, though they be of lower birth – women, vaiśyas [merchants] and śūdras [workers] – can attain the supreme destination."
- **Purport point:** "in devotional service there is no distinction between the lower and higher classes of people";
  "anyone can take to it."
- **Use:** in the talk, for K.6 and K.11.

**SB 2.4.18** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/4/18/
> "Kirāta, Hūṇa, Āndhra, Pulinda, Pulkaśa, Ābhīra, Śumbha, Yavana, members of the Khasa races and even others addicted to sinful acts can be purified by taking shelter of the devotees of the Lord, due to His being the supreme power. I beg to offer my respectful obeisances unto Him."
- **Purport point:** Srila Prabhupada identifies the peoples named, including Greeks (Pulinda), Huns and Yavanas, and
  the conclusion that all can be purified by the devotees' shelter.
- **Application (preacher's):** The Bhagavatam itself, five thousand years ago, expects the message to reach every
  people. Westerners in dhotis are this verse happening.
- **Use:** in the talk, for K.6.

**BG 3.8** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/8/
> "Perform your prescribed duty, for doing so is better than not working. One cannot even maintain one's physical body without work."
- **Purport point:** "So-called renunciation for the purpose of maintenance is never approved by the Lord, nor by any
  religious scripture"; one should not become "a so-called transcendentalist, renouncing work and living at the cost
  of others."
- **Application (preacher's):** The answer to "ISKCON makes boys leave their studies". The Gita, and therefore
  ISKCON, tells the student to study and the earner to earn.
- **Use:** in the talk, for K.5.

**BG 18.66** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/18/66/
> "Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me. I shall deliver you from all sinful reactions. Do not fear."
- **Application (preacher's):** Often quoted *against* ISKCON as proof of intolerance ("abandon all religion"). The
  purport: give up the *processes* (knowledge, yoga, ritual) as ends and take their goal directly. It is a verse about
  the goal of religion, not a verse against anyone's religion. Say so.
- **Use:** in K.8.

**SB 1.1.2** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/1/2/
> "Completely rejecting all religious activities which are materially motivated, this Bhāgavata Purāṇa propounds the highest truth… for the welfare of all."
- **Purport point:** "religion is the extra function of the human being"; religion performed for gain is "so-called
  religion".
- **Use:** background for F.8: the Bhagavatam's dharma is "for the welfare of all", not a community's.

**SB 1.2.6** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/6/
> "The supreme occupation [dharma] for all humanity is that by which men can attain to loving devotional service unto the transcendent Lord. Such devotional service must be unmotivated and uninterrupted to completely satisfy the self."
- **Application (preacher's):** "For all humanity" (*sarva-puṁsām*): the scriptural basis for saying ISKCON is not a
  sect. Pair with the Introduction to the Gita: sanatana-dharma "does not refer to any sectarian process of religion".
- **Use:** in the talk; the verse to remember.

**CC Adi 9.41** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/adi/9/41/
> "One who has taken his birth as a human being in the land of India [Bhārata-varṣa] should make his life successful and work for the benefit of all other people."
- **Purport point:** Sri Caitanya "is addressing not only Bengalis but all the inhabitants of India"; India's
  inheritance of spiritual culture is to be shared.
- **Application (preacher's):** The answer to "ISKCON is foreign": it is India's own instruction to itself, carried
  out. Srila Prabhupada left Calcutta at sixty-nine to do what this verse says. **Caution:** the purport also contains
  sharp remarks about India's leaders of the 1970s; do not read them out; they are not the point.
- **Use:** in the talk; the third answer.

**CC Adi 17.21** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/adi/17/21/
> "In this Age of Kali there is no other means, no other means, no other means for self-realization than chanting the holy name, chanting the holy name, chanting the holy name of Lord Hari."
- **Context:** Sri Caitanya quotes the *Brhan-naradiya Purana* to the Kazi during the civil-disobedience kirtana at
  Navadvipa.
- **Application (preacher's):** Why devotees chant in the street: the scripture of this age prescribes it, and Sri
  Caitanya did it publicly against a ban. Street kirtana is not a cult's eccentricity; it is the yuga-dharma.
- **Use:** in the talk.

**SB 11.5.32, SB 12.3.51 and SB 12.3.52:** moved to R.1 (deeper verses); the talk uses CC Adi 17.21 for public kirtana.

**SB 10.33.26-27**, **SB 10.33.29**, **SB 10.33.30** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/33/26-27/ · *BBT translation, completed by Srila Prabhupada's disciples.*
> 10.33.26–27: "…Indeed, He is the original speaker, follower and guardian of moral laws. How, then, could He have violated them by touching other men's wives?"
> 10.33.30: "One who is not a great controller should never imitate the behavior of ruling personalities, even mentally…"
- **Context:** Pariksit asks, on behalf of doubters in the assembly, the exact question modern critics ask (purport
  to 26–27); Sukadeva answers (29–40).
- **Purport point (10.33.30):** "we should not imitate Lord Kṛṣṇa's activities with the gopīs… He also descends to
  demonstrate that He is God and we are not."
- **Application (preacher's):** The scripture raises the objection itself and answers it; the preacher's job at VL3 is
  to show that, not to explain the rasa-lila. Beyond this, refer to seniors (sps-33).
- **Use:** only in K.9.

## F. Philosophical explanation

The ten misconceptions, each with the fact, the source and the tone. *Application (preacher's):* the selection is
mine, from what Delhi students report hearing; no statistics are claimed.

1. **"ISKCON is a new sect / an American cult from the 1960s."** The society was registered in New York in July 1966
   (Srila Prabhupada's incorporation of ISKCON; S.B. Keshava Swami, "Incorporation of ISKCON in New York", O). The
   *teaching* is the Gaudiya Vaisnava parampara, which the Introduction to *Bhagavad-gita As It Is* traces through
   thirty-two names from Krsna to Srila Prabhupada; BG 4.2 is the principle that knowledge comes by succession and that
   successions are re-established. **Tone:** "New organisation, old teaching: like a new school teaching an old
   syllabus."
2. **"ISKCON disrespects other gods; it says only Krsna."** BG 9.23: worshippers of other gods "actually worship only
   Me… but they do so in a wrong way"; SB 4.31.14 purport: Srila Prabhupada names this very question and answers it with
   the root-and-tree. Devotees offer respect to Siva, Ganesa, Durga as great servants of the Lord; they direct *worship*
   to the root. **Tone:** never mock a family's deity; the verse says their faith reaches Krsna.
3. **"Hinduism accepts many paths; ISKCON is narrow."** The Introduction to the Gita: sanatana-dharma "does not refer
   to any sectarian process of religion" but to "the eternal function of the eternal living entities in relationship
   with the eternal Supreme Lord"; SB 1.2.6: the supreme dharma "for all humanity". ISKCON teaches *one goal*, love of
   God, and the Gita's *one conclusion*, surrender to Krsna (BG 18.66), while honouring every genuine step towards it.
   **Tone:** "Narrow about the goal, generous about people."
4. **"It is a money machine: marble temples, selling books, donation drives."** BG 9.27 purport: surplus "should be
   utilized in furthering the Kṛṣṇa consciousness movement"; NOI 2: collecting more than required spoils devotion. Srila
   Prabhupada founded the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust so that book income funds printing and temples; the exact formula is
   **Requires source review** (BBT's published trust terms). How your temple is registered and whether it publishes
   audited accounts: ask your temple authority (Requires source review; do not assert it from this page).
   **Application (preacher's):** invite the questioner to ask the temple for its annual report rather than defending
   figures you do not know. Prasadam distribution (Food for Life) may be mentioned as a visible use of funds; give no
   numbers you have not checked.
5. **"ISKCON brainwashes boys, makes them leave studies and family, turns them into monks."** BG 3.8 and purport:
   prescribed duty is to be done; "so-called renunciation for the purpose of maintenance is never approved". Srila
   Prabhupada, Morning Walk, Chicago, 8 Jul 1975 (Verified, vedabase.io/en/library/transcripts/750708mwchi/): asked by
   a devotee who wanted to be free of his family to preach, "Preaching cannot be checked by anything… In any
   circumstances you can preach", and "You can serve Kṛṣṇa in any capacity". (The *Back to Godhead* edit of this walk,
   in the Preaching Encyclopedia, puts "Kṛṣṇa consciousness is not hindered by family life" in his mouth; in the
   transcript that sentence is Tamala Krsna's, so do not quote it as Prabhupada's.) IYF's own criteria for every level
   are class attendance, course assessments, chanting and facilitator meetings, not renunciation
   (`research/batch-criteria.md`). The brahmacari ashrama exists and is honourable; it is chosen by a few adults after
   years, with guidance (BSS, DPS-56). **Tone:** "Ask them to meet my facilitator" (VL3-13).
6. **"It is a foreign thing: Western Hindus in dhotis."** CC Adi 9.41: India's own mandate to share its spiritual
   culture; Srila Prabhupada was a Calcutta-born acharya who went West at sixty-nine. SB 2.4.18 and BG 9.32: the
   Bhagavatam expects Yavanas, Hunas and Greeks to be purified by the devotees' shelter; the foreign devotee is the
   prediction fulfilled. **Tone:** gratitude, not defensiveness.
7. **"They dance and sing in the streets like a cult."** CC Adi 17.21: "no other means" in Kali-yuga than the holy
   name. Sri Caitanya led public kirtana against the Kazi's ban. **Tone:** "Public kirtana is five hundred years
   older than ISKCON."
8. **"ISKCON converts people" / "ISKCON is anti-Hindu" / "ISKCON is Hindutva."** Three contradictory charges, all
   answered by the same fact: ISKCON teaches sanatana-dharma, which is nobody's property (Gita Introduction; SB 1.2.6
   "for all humanity"; SB 1.1.2 "for the welfare of all"), and it is a registered religious and educational society,
   not a political body. Devotees of every background chant the same name without changing their passport or their
   family. **Application (preacher's) and caution (sensitive):** take no political side in class; if pressed, say
   "ISKCON has no party, and I don't speak for one." For the question as devotees discuss it, hear Chaitanya Charan's
   Q&A (O) before teaching; do not improvise.
9. **"Krsna's dealings with the gopis are immoral; ISKCON glorifies them."** Pariksit Maharaja asked the identical
   question (SB 10.33.26–27, on behalf of the doubters present); Sukadeva's answer (10.33.29–36) is that the Lord is not
   bound by the rules He makes for us and that no one should imitate Him "even mentally" (10.33.30). At VL3 the answer
   stops here: "The Bhagavatam asked your question first, and answered it; and it forbids imitation." Anything further
   is sps-33, with senior review. **Tone:** calm; never defensive, never explanatory beyond this.
10. **"ISKCON had scandals: gurus who fell, abuse in its schools in the 1970s–80s."** *Application (preacher's),
    pastoral guidance:* this is true, and a VL3 preacher must not deny it or change the subject. The honest frame:
    some leaders fell, and children in some ISKCON boarding schools (gurukulas) of that period were harmed; ISKCON has
    acknowledged this publicly and established a child-protection office with published policies. Any further claim
    (dates, numbers, what changed in the guru system) waits for the documents. **Requires source
    review:** the head is to confirm which official ISKCON documents (GBC statements, Child Protection Office policy)
    IYF cites (Stage 2 §2.5, item 5). Until then, the preacher says: "Yes. It happened; the movement has said so and
    acted; I can show you its published statements." Scripture's own frame: a practitioner's fall does not falsify the
    teaching (BG 9.30–31, taught at dps-01), *and* that verse is never used to excuse harm to others (its purport: "No
    one should take advantage of this verse and commit nonsense and think that he is still a devotee"). **Tone:** sober honesty; no minimising; no details you cannot source.
11. **Two more, briefly.** *"It is for Brahmins / it is anti-women."* BG 9.32 and SB 2.4.18: devotional service is
    open to all, and all "can attain the supreme destination". **Caution:** BG 9.32's translation itself says "lower
    birth – women, vaiśyas… and śūdras", and a critic will read that phrase back to you; quote the conclusion, not the
    list, and take the wording question to a senior. Srila Prabhupada gave brahminical initiation irrespective of birth (Partially verified: Lilamrta;
    widely documented). *"Why do you worship a man (Prabhupada)?"* BG 4.34 and purport: the spiritual master in
    succession is honoured as the Lord's representative; devotees do not say he is God (vl3-07, Gurv-astaka verse 7).
12. **How to say any of it.** From VL3-13 and BG 17.15: truthful, pleasing, beneficial, not agitating. One fact, one
    source, one sentence of warmth, then stop. The student who argues longest convinces least. Questions he cannot
    answer go to his facilitator (VL3-08) with the words "Good question; let me find out properly."
13. **Pitch for VL3.** Practical and encouraging: ten answers short enough to remember, each with a verse; the
    relief of being allowed to say "I don't know, I'll ask"; and the assurance that the hardest questions (9, 10) are
    not his to carry alone.

## H. Opening and engagement

**H.1 How to start (first 5 minutes)**
1. Greet, say your name, and say today is about answering questions kindly, not winning arguments.
2. Say that questions about faith are normal anywhere in the world; people who ask are usually worried or curious, not hostile.
3. Ask H.2 question 1; write every answer on the board, including unkind ones, without comment.
4. Group the answers into money, other gods, family and study, public practice, and "hard questions".
5. Say that two kinds of question are always taken to a senior, and begin F.1.

**H.2 Opening questions** (about the room's own life)
1. What has anyone said to you about this path or about any faith you follow or know?
2. Think of a time a friend's remark about something you cared about stung. What did you want to say back?
3. What would help you answer calmly: a short sentence, a source, a pause?


- **Opening question:** "What has anyone said to you about ISKCON since you started coming?" Write every answer up
  without comment, including the unkind ones. Then: "Each of these has an honest answer. Some are short. Two are for

## K. Anticipated questions and objections

> **Before you teach:** confirm with your temple authority (a) the current answer on finances you may give and (b)
> the official documents you may point to for F.10. Write both on your card. Do not improvise either.

1. **"Isn't ISKCON just a 1960s American cult?"** F.1: BG 4.2; the Gita's parampara; registered 1966 by an Indian
   acharya. "New society; teaching older than the society by thousands of years."
2. **"Why do you reject Siva, Ganesa, Durga?"** We don't; we respect them as great servants of the Lord (BG 9.23: their
   worshippers' faith "actually" reaches Krsna). We direct worship to the root (SB 4.31.14). Never mock.
3. **"Hinduism is about many paths. Why is yours the only one?"** One goal, love of God, for all humanity (SB 1.2.6);
   the Gita's conclusion is surrender (BG 18.66), and the purport says that verse is about the *goal* of religion, not
   against anyone's religion. We honour every genuine step.
4. **"Where does all the money go?"** Into books, prasadam and temples (BG 9.27 purport); collecting more than needed
   is itself condemned (NOI 2). "Ask the temple for its accounts." Give no figure you haven't seen.
5. **"They'll make you leave your studies / become a monk."** BG 3.8: duty is to be done; Srila Prabhupada: "Kṛṣṇa
   consciousness" can be served "in any capacity" (Srila Prabhupada, Chicago, 8 Jul 1975). Nothing in IYF's courses
   asks anyone to leave studies (batch-criteria). Invite the parent to meet the facilitator.
6. **"It's a foreigners' thing."** CC Adi 9.41 and SB 2.4.18: India's own mandate; the Bhagavatam expects Greeks and
   Huns to join. The founder was from Calcutta.
7. **"Why dance in the street?"** CC Adi 17.21: the prescribed practice of this age, done
   publicly by Sri Caitanya Himself.
8. **"Do you convert people?" / "Are you a political outfit?"** Nobody changes community or passport to chant; ISKCON
   has no party. Say that and no more; politics stays out of the class (sensitive).
9. **"What about Krsna and the gopis?"** "Pariksit asked exactly that (SB 10.33.26–27). Sukadeva's answer: the Lord
   is not bound by our rules, and we must never imitate Him (10.33.30). If you want more than that, I'll bring a senior
   who can explain it properly." Then stop.
10. **"What about the scandals and the abuse cases?"** "Yes. Some leaders fell and children were harmed in some ISKCON
    schools decades ago. The movement has acknowledged it and set up child protection. I can show you its
    published statements." Then refer to the documents the head has approved (Requires source review). Never deny,
    minimise or excuse; never quote BG 9.30 as a defence of harm.
11. **"Isn't it casteist / anti-women?"** BG 9.32; SB 2.4.18; Srila Prabhupada initiated without regard to birth
    (Partially verified). Where devotees have fallen short, say so; the teaching is clear.
12. **"Why worship Prabhupada? He was a man."** We honour him as the acharya in the succession who brought the teaching
    (BG 4.34 purport); we do not call him God (Gurv-astaka verse 7 says the guru is honoured *as good as* the Lord
    because he is the Lord's dear servant).

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary

ISKCON (the International Society for Krishna Consciousness) was registered in 1966 by Srila Prabhupada, a teacher from Calcutta, but what it teaches is far older: the Bhagavad-gita received through an unbroken succession of teachers (BG 4.2), which the Introduction to *Bhagavad-gita As It Is* lists from Krsna to Srila Prabhupada. It teaches *sanatana-dharma*, the eternal function of every soul, "for all humanity" (SB 1.2.6), so it is not a sect and converts no one to a new community. Devotees worship Krsna as the root of everything, which is why they respect other gods but direct worship to the root (SB 4.31.14; BG 9.23). BG 18.66, "abandon all varieties of religion", is not a verse against other religions; its purport explains that one gives up the processes as ends in themselves and takes the goal directly.

Money is engaged in books, prasadam (food offered to Krsna) and temples (BG 9.27), and collecting more than needed is condemned (NOI 2). The Gita tells students to study and earners to earn (BG 3.8). India's own scriptures expect people from every land to join (CC Adi 9.41; SB 2.4.18), so the movement is not a foreign import. Public kirtana (congregational chanting) is the practice prescribed for this age, done publicly by Sri Caitanya Himself (CC Adi 17.21).

Two questions you will hear, about Krsna and the gopis and about past scandals, have honest answers that your seniors should give; your part is to say "yes, that is a real question, let me bring someone who can answer it properly." For every other objection: listen, thank the person, answer briefly with one source, and keep the friendship, speaking truthfully, pleasingly and beneficially (BG 17.15).

## S2. Key ideas

- New society (1966), old teaching: knowledge received by succession (BG 4.2).
- Sanatana-dharma is for all humanity; nobody changes community to chant (SB 1.2.6).
- Water the root: worship Krsna, respect the demigods as His servants (SB 4.31.14; BG 9.23).
- Money is engaged, not avoided; over-collecting is condemned (BG 9.27; NOI 2).
- Duty is to be done: study, work, family (BG 3.8).
- Public kirtana is this age's prescribed practice (CC Adi 17.21).
- Two questions are referred, honestly: the gopis; past scandals.

## S3. Verses to remember

- **BG 4.2:** "This supreme science was thus received through the chain of disciplic succession…" *For me:* I am part of something older than the society I joined.
- **SB 4.31.14:** "As pouring water on the root of a tree energizes the trunk, branches, twigs and everything else… simply worshiping the Supreme Personality of Godhead through devotional service automatically satisfies the demigods…" *For me:* I don't reject anyone's god; I water the root.
- **SB 1.2.6:** "The supreme occupation [dharma] for all humanity is that by which men can attain to loving devotional service unto the transcendent Lord…" *For me:* this is for everyone, and so it is not a sect.

## S4. Words to know

- **ISKCON:** International Society for Krishna Consciousness, founded by Srila Prabhupada in 1966.
- **Parampara:** the disciplic succession through which knowledge is received (BG 4.2).
- **Sanatana-dharma:** the eternal function of the soul in relation to God; not a sectarian religion.
- **Demigods:** the Lord's empowered servants (Siva, Ganesa, Durga and others), respected, not worshipped as the Supreme.
- **Sankirtana:** congregational chanting of the holy name, the practice prescribed for this age.

## S5. Questions and answers

1. **"Isn't ISKCON an American cult?"** New society, registered in 1966 by an Indian acharya; teaching received by succession from the Gita itself (BG 4.2).
2. **"Why do you reject other gods?"** We don't. We respect them as the Lord's servants and worship the root (SB 4.31.14; BG 9.23).
3. **"Where does the money go?"** Books, prasadam, temples (BG 9.27 purport). Over-collecting is condemned (NOI 2). Ask the temple for its accounts.
4. **"Will they make you leave your studies?"** No. The Gita says duty is to be done (BG 3.8), and nothing in IYF's courses asks you to leave studies. Invite your parents to meet your facilitator.
5. **"What about Krsna and the gopis?" / "What about the scandals?"** Honest questions. Say: "Pariksit asked the first one himself (SB 10.33.26–27) and the Bhagavatam forbids imitation (10.33.30). On the second: yes, it happened, and the movement has acknowledged it and set up child protection. Let me bring someone who can answer properly."
