# Cooperation: "your love for me will be shown by how you cooperate"

> **TRD · up to 180 min · review: drafted (30 Oct 2026).** Group A. **Source note:** the title sentence is not found in
> any letter on vedabase. Its verified source is the BBT *Srila Prabhupada-lilamrta* (vol. 2 ch. 52), which records
> Srila Prabhupada saying it to Tamala Krsna Goswami in Vrindavana in 1977: "Your love for me will be shown by how much

> used here, cited as the *Lilamrta*'s record. The letter claim in research/trd-topics.md is marked **Requires source

## B. Why this topic belongs at this level

An ashram is a team of men who each gave up everything and therefore each have strong convictions. Disagreement is
normal; what Srila Prabhupada asked for is cooperation in spite of it. His 1977 sentence makes cooperation the measure of
love for him; his 1972 letter to Vrindavana says "Stop this fighting." Residents need both, and the scripture behind them.

## C. Central message

Srila Prabhupada told his leaders in 1977 that their love for him would be shown by how much they cooperate to keep the
institution together after he is gone (*Lilamrta* 2.52). In 1977 he also wrote, "I am only one person, but because all of
you have kindly cooperated with me, this movement has now become a success all over the world" (letter, 6 May 1977). The
sages at Naimisaranya show the model: strong men assembled for one sacrifice, for the good of all (SB 1.1.4). A resident
cooperates not because he agrees, but because the mission and the founder asked him to.

## D. Learning outcomes

Participants should be able to:
1. Quote Srila Prabhupada's 1977 sentence with its verified source, and two of his letters on cooperation.
2. Explain from SB 1.1.4 and its purport why strong, independent sages assembled, and what that models.
3. State the Gita's cooperation principle (BG 3.11) and the CC Adi 9.41 purport's hope of "cooperation among the Indian people and among the other people of the world".
4. Describe how the six exchanges (NOI 4) and respect by level (NOI 5) make cooperation possible between strong personalities.
5. Choose one present disagreement and decide one cooperative step, with CC Antya 20.21 as the mood.

## E. Scriptural foundation

**SB 1.1.4** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/1/4/
> "Once, in a holy place in the forest of Naimiṣāraṇya, great sages headed by the sage Śaunaka assembled to perform a great thousand-year sacrifice for the satisfaction of the Lord and His devotees."
- **Context:** The opening of the Bhagavatam's narrative; the sages gather to hear Suta Gosvami.
- **Teaching:** Great sages, each complete in himself, assembled under one head (Saunaka) for one long sacrifice for the Lord's and the devotees' satisfaction.
- **Purport point:** "The great sages are always anxious to do good to the people in general… They are sincere friends to all living entities, and at the risk of great personal inconvenience they are always engaged in the service of the Lord for the good of all people." Then the tree: "Lord Visnu is just like a great tree, and all others… are like branches, twigs and leaves of that tree. By pouring water on the root of the tree, all the parts of the tree are automatically nourished."
- **Application (preacher's):** The sages did not each hold a private sacrifice. One head, one purpose, a thousand years: the picture of a team that cooperates for the good of all. The root-watering image says why cooperation does not require agreement on every branch: serve the root.
- **Use:** in the talk; the central verse.

**BG 3.11** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/11/
> "The demigods, being pleased by sacrifices, will also please you, and thus, by cooperation between men and demigods, prosperity will reign for all."
- **Teaching:** Srila Prabhupada's translation itself uses the word "cooperation": the Lord's arrangement for prosperity is mutual service between the Lord's agents and men, centred on sacrifice.
- **Use:** in the talk; read the purport on vedabase before adding a purport point.

**CC Adi 9.41** — Verified (trd-01). The purport's hope: if India trains preachers, "People will accept it, there will be cooperation among the Indian people and among the other people of the world, and the mission of Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu will then be fulfilled… we are very happy that American and European boys and girls are seriously cooperating with this movement."
- **Use:** in the talk: cooperation is written into the purpose of the mission.

**NOI 4** — Verified (trd-06). The six exchanges; the purport: "The life of the Krsna conscious society is nourished by these six types of loving exchange among the members."
- **Use:** in the talk, as the mechanism: strong personalities cooperate by giving, accepting, opening their minds and inquiring, not by agreeing.

**NOI 5** — Verified (trd-02). Respect for each devotee according to his status.
- **Use:** in the talk: cooperation between a senior and a junior is not symmetry; it is respect in both directions by level.

**CC Antya 20.21** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/20/21/
> "One who thinks himself lower than the grass, who is more tolerant than a tree, and who does not expect personal honor but is always prepared to give all respect to others can very easily always chant the holy name of the Lord."
- **Teaching:** The third Siksastaka verse: humility, tolerance, and giving respect while not expecting it.
- **Use:** in the talk, as the mood that makes cooperation between strong men possible.

**Srila Prabhupada's words on cooperation, Verified on vedabase:**
- ***Srila Prabhupada-lilamrta* vol. 2 ch. 52 ("I Have Done My Part"), Vrindavana, 1977** · https://vedabase.io/en/library/spl/2/52/ : when Tamala Krsna Goswami said the GBC men would come "because they love you", "'Your love for me,' said Srila Prabhupada, 'will be shown by how much you cooperate to keep this institution together after I am gone.'" (Recorded by Satsvarupa dasa Goswami; the chapter also notes Tamala Krsna repeating it to the arriving Godbrothers.)
- **Letter to Sumati Morarjee, New York, 26 November 1966** · https://vedabase.io/en/library/letters/letter-to-sumati-morarjee-6/ : "I expect full cooperation of all devotees of Krishna in India specially in Bombay in this mighty progress. ## F. Philosophical explanation

1. **Cooperation is built into the mission.** The CC Adi 9.41 purport expects "cooperation among the Indian people and among the other people of the world" as the way Lord Caitanya's mission is fulfilled; the 1966 letter "expects full cooperation"; the 1977 letter credits the movement's success to it. For Srila Prabhupada, cooperation was not a management value; it was how the order of Lord Caitanya gets done.
2. **The test of love.** The 1977 sentence turns an abstract word into a measurable one: love for the founder is shown by cooperating to keep the institution together. A resident who loves Srila Prabhupada privately but will not work with the devotee next to him has, by Srila Prabhupada's own measure, something still to show.
3. **The model (SB 1.1.4).** Great sages, under one head, in one place, for one purpose, for a thousand years, for the good of all. Nobody there was weak or unqualified; cooperation was the choice of the strong. The purport's tree says why: water the root (serve Krishna) and every branch is nourished; argue about branches and the tree dries.
4. **The Lord's own arrangement (BG 3.11).** Even the universe runs on mutual service centred on sacrifice; "prosperity will reign for all" by cooperation. A temple that does not cooperate within itself contradicts the order of the world it preaches about.
5. **How strong personalities cooperate (NOI 4, NOI 5).** Not by agreeing. By the six exchanges: giving and accepting, opening the mind and inquiring (dps-36 gives these among devotees), and by respect according to level in both directions. Disagreement handled inside the six exchanges is cooperation; disagreement handled outside them is "this fighting".
6. **The mood (CC Antya 20.21).** Humbler than grass, more tolerant than a tree, giving respect and not expecting it: without this, strong men can only compete. With it, the same strength serves the sacrifice.
7. **Misunderstandings.** (a) "Cooperation means silence": the sages asked Suta questions; the exchanges include revealing the mind. Cooperation speaks, within the structure. (b) "Cooperate only with those I respect": NOI 5 asks for respect by level toward every devotee. (c) "I cooperate with Prabhupada, not with these people": the 1977 sentence says the opposite; cooperation with them is the way of showing it.

**H.2 Opening questions**

## I. Core teaching points

1. **His words (1966–1977).** "I expect full cooperation"; "Anyone who will cooperate with this movement will be transcendentally benefited"; "always cooperate with each other at all times… Stop this fighting"; "because all of you have kindly cooperated with me, this movement has now become a success"; and the 1977 test of love. Example: the Vrindavana letter was written to devotees quarrelling over a temple project; the words are for exactly our situations. Transition: "What does the Bhagavatam show?"
2. **The sages (SB 1.1.4).** Strong, independent, assembled under one head for one sacrifice "for the good of all"; water the root. Example: a kitchen, a book table and an accounts office arguing about priority are branches; the Sunday programme's purpose (giving Krishna) is the root. Transition: "Even the universe works this way."
3. **Cooperation in the design (BG 3.11; CC Adi 9.41).** Prosperity by cooperation between men and the Lord's agents; Lord Caitanya's mission fulfilled by cooperation among peoples. Example: a resident who preaches BG 3.11 at a programme and refuses cooperation at the next day's meeting has contradicted his own class. Transition: "But we are strong personalities."
4. **The mechanism (NOI 4, NOI 5).** Give, accept, reveal, inquire; respect by level both ways. Example: a junior with a better idea reveals it respectfully to the senior and inquires; the senior accepts the gift and explains his reasons. Both have cooperated, whatever is decided. Transition: "And the mood."
5. **The mood (CC Antya 20.21).** Humbler than grass, more tolerant than a tree, respect given and not expected. Example: the one who gives way on a branch to protect the root is not weak; he is the sage. Transition to L.

## J. Stories and illustrations

**Naimisaranya (SB 1.1.4, with the purport's details).** In the forest of Naimisaranya, which the Vayaviya Tantra
describes as the hub of a great wheel Brahma contemplated to enclose the universe, and where the Varaha Purana says
sacrifice curtails the strength of demoniac people, great sages headed by Saunaka assembled for a thousand-year
sacrifice for the satisfaction of the Lord and His devotees. Devotional lesson (purport): they are "sincere friends to
all living entities" who serve "at the risk of great personal inconvenience" for the good of all.

**Vrindavana, 1972 (letter of 11 August, Verified).** While approving Saurabha's temple plans, Srila Prabhupada heard of
trouble between devotees and wrote: "always cooperate with each other at all times… Better to cooperate all of you and do
something tangible for Krsna. Stop this fighting." He then gave them a positive programme: "Have a European preaching
center and try to enlist all the hippies and tourists who come to Vrndavana. Give them nice prasadam, engage them in
chanting, cleaning the temple, reading our books." Lesson: his remedy for fighting was a shared service.

**Vrindavana, 1977 (*Lilamrta* 2.52, Verified text).** Tamala Krsna Goswami wanted to confirm that Srila Prabhupada
really wished all the GBC men to come from across the world, and said, "Because they love you, I am sure they will all
want to come and be with you." Srila Prabhupada replied: "Your love for me will be shown by how much you cooperate to
keep this institution together after I am gone." Lesson: he defined love for him by cooperation, at the end.

## K. Anticipated questions and objections

1. **"I have heard the quote as 'how you cooperate with each other' / 'to push on this movement'. Which is right?"** Many wordings circulate in Vyasa-puja offerings and talks. The one verified in a BBT book is the *Lilamrta*'s: "how much you cooperate to keep this institution together after I am gone." Use that, with its source. No letter with the sentence has been found on vedabase.
2. **"Cooperation with a decision I think is wrong: isn't that dishonesty?"** No: say what you think, through the six exchanges (reveal, inquire), to the right person, then cooperate with the decision unless it breaks a principle. Honesty is in the speaking; cooperation is in the doing. (For principle-breaking orders, see dps-03 and trd-16.)
3. **"The other person will not cooperate. What then?"** Cooperate anyway in what is yours (SB 1.1.4: "at the risk of great personal inconvenience"), keep respect by level (NOI 5), and bring the matter to the authority; trd-19 gives the steps.
4. **"Isn't 'keep this institution together' about GBC men, not residents?"** It was said to a GBC man about the institution; the institution is kept together, or not, at every table where two residents must agree. The 1972 letter was to a temple's devotees.
5. **"Strong personalities are an asset; won't cooperation make everyone bland?"** The sages were not bland. Strength serves the sacrifice when the root is watered; it fights when each waters his own branch.

## L. Case studies

**Case 1: Two programmes, one hall (Invented case for discussion).** The college-preaching team and the congregation
team both want the main hall on Saturday evening; both leaders are senior, both have grown their programmes, and the
temple president has asked them to "sort it out". Each has begun telling juniors the other is "not serious".
- **Dilemma:** Both services are good; the fighting is not.
- **Principles:** SB 1.1.4 purport (water the root); NOI 4 (reveal, inquire); NOI 5 (respect by level, here between equals); the 1972 letter ("Stop this fighting").
- **Questions:** What is the root both programmes serve? Which of the six exchanges have the two leaders stopped doing? What single act this week would be cooperation, whatever the hall decision?
- **Analysis:** Speaking about the other to juniors is the fight; speaking to the other is the exchange. A shared service (joint prasadam, alternate weeks, or a combined event) is the remedy the 1972 letter models.
- **Takeaway:** Decide the hall quickly; repair the exchange permanently.

**Case 2: The junior with the better plan (Invented case for discussion).** Keshav, two years in, sees that the book
table's placement loses half the footfall. His senior has used that spot for ten years. Keshav has told three other
brahmacaris, not the senior.
- **Principles:** NOI 4 (reveal to the right person, inquire); NOI 5 (respect by level); CC Antya 20.21 (give respect, do not expect it).
- **Takeaway:** The gift is the idea; the exchange is offering it to the senior and asking his reasons. Whatever he decides, Keshav has cooperated; the corridor conversations were not.

**M.2 Activity (30 min): "Root and branches."** Groups of four. Each group takes one real but unnamed temple
disagreement (service, not persons) and draws the SB 1.1.4 tree: what is the root (the purpose both sides serve) and
which branches are being argued about. Then each group writes one cooperative act that waters the root this week, naming
which of the six exchanges it uses. Report back in one sentence.

**M.3 Reflection (private).** "In which present disagreement am I speaking about a devotee rather than to him? What is the one exchange I owe him?"

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary

In 1977, in Vrindavana, Tamala Krsna Goswami told Srila Prabhupada that the leaders would come to him from around the
world "because they love you". Srila Prabhupada replied: "Your love for me will be shown by how much you cooperate to
keep this institution together after I am gone" (recorded in *Srila Prabhupada-lilamrta*, vol. 2 ch. 52). The sentence
is often repeated in other words; this is the wording a BBT book records.

He had asked for cooperation for eleven years. In 1966, from New York: "I expect full cooperation of all devotees of
Krishna in India." In 1970: "Anyone who will cooperate with this movement will be transcendentally benefited." In 1972,
to devotees quarrelling in Vrindavana: "always cooperate with each other at all times… Better to cooperate all of you and
do something tangible for Krsna. Stop this fighting." In May 1977: "I am only one person, but because all of you have
kindly cooperated with me, this movement has now become a success all over the world."

The Bhagavatam opens with the model. Great sages headed by Saunaka assembled at Naimisaranya for a thousand-year
sacrifice for the satisfaction of the Lord and His devotees (SB 1.1.4). Srila Prabhupada writes that they were "sincere
friends to all living entities" serving "at the risk of great personal inconvenience… for the good of all people", and
gives the picture of a tree: water the root, and every branch is nourished. The Gita says the world itself runs this way:
"by cooperation between men and demigods, prosperity will reign for all" (BG 3.11). Lord Caitanya's mission is fulfilled
when "there will be cooperation among the Indian people and among the other people of the world" (CC Adi 9.41 purport).

Strong personalities cooperate not by agreeing but through the six exchanges, giving, accepting, revealing the mind,
inquiring, sharing prasadam (NOI 4), with respect for each devotee by his level (NOI 5), in the mood of Lord Caitanya's
verse: humbler than grass, more tolerant than a tree, giving respect and not expecting it (CC Antya 20.21).

## S2. Key ideas

- Love for Srila Prabhupada is shown by cooperating to keep the institution together (*Lilamrta* 2.52).
- He asked for cooperation from 1966 to 1977 and credited the movement's success to it.
- The sages at Naimisaranya: strong men, one head, one sacrifice, for the good of all (SB 1.1.4).
- Water the root (serve Krishna) and the branches are nourished (SB 1.1.4 purport).
- Strong personalities cooperate through the six exchanges and respect by level (NOI 4, NOI 5), humbly (CC Antya 20.21).

## S3. Verses to remember

- **SB 1.1.4:** "great sages headed by the sage Saunaka assembled to perform a great thousand-year sacrifice for the satisfaction of the Lord and His devotees." *For me:* strong people can assemble for one purpose.
- **BG 3.11:** "by cooperation between men and demigods, prosperity will reign for all." *For me:* cooperation is the Lord's own arrangement.
- **CC Antya 20.21:** "lower than the grass… more tolerant than a tree… always prepared to give all respect to others." *For me:* the mood that makes cooperation possible.

## S4. Words to know

- **Istagosthi:** a devotees' meeting to discuss service openly.
- **Naimisaranya:** the forest where the sages assembled to hear the Bhagavatam.
- **GBC:** the Governing Body Commission, ISKCON's leading body; the men Srila Prabhupada spoke to in 1977.
- **Six exchanges:** giving, accepting, revealing the mind, inquiring, accepting and offering prasadam (NOI 4).
- **Respect by level:** honouring each devotee according to his status (NOI 5).

## S5. Questions and answers

1. **Which wording of the 1977 sentence should I use?** The one in the BBT *Lilamrta*: "how much you cooperate to keep this institution together after I am gone." Other wordings circulate; no letter with the sentence has been found.
2. **If I think a decision is wrong, is cooperating dishonest?** No. Say what you think to the right person, respectfully; then cooperate with the decision unless it breaks a principle. Honesty is in the speaking, cooperation in the doing.
3. **What if the other devotee will not cooperate?** Do your part anyway, keep respect, and bring it to your authority.
4. **Was the sentence only for leaders?** It was said to a leader about the institution; the institution is held together wherever two devotees must agree. The 1972 letter was to a temple's devotees.
5. **Will cooperation make everyone the same?** The sages were not the same. Strength serves when the root is watered; it fights when each waters his own branch.
