## B. Why this topic belongs at this level

Builds on trd-01: that page gives the purpose (benefit others by giving Krishna); this page gives Lord Caitanya's exact
method for doing it with everyone one meets. For a resident the question is practical: the cook, the guard, the
shopkeeper, the parent at the gate, the student in the lift. The verse says "whomever you meet".

| Level | Treatment |
|---|---|
| VL1 | Not covered |
| VL2 | "You can share what you have received": one sentence of CC Madhya 7.128 and the invitation to tell a friend |
| VL3 | The Kurma story and the verse as the practice: stay where you are, chant, tell others |
| SPS | "Become a spiritual master by My order": what the order is and is not; NOI 5 on respecting devotees by level |

| BSS | A brahmacari's daily contacts as preaching; sense control makes the preacher credible (BG 3.21) |

**Not done here:** why we preach (trd-01); Srila Prabhupada's personal mood (trd-03); books (trd-05); the follow-up
after the first meeting (trd-06).

## C. Central message

Lord Caitanya told the brahmana Kurma: instruct everyone you meet to follow Krishna's orders as given in the Gita and
the Bhagavatam; in this way, by My order, become a spiritual master and liberate everyone in this land
(CC Madhya 7.128). Srila Prabhupada calls this "the sublime mission of the International Society for Krishna
Consciousness". A resident does it with every person he meets, by repeating Krishna's instructions, not his own.

## D. Learning outcomes

Participants should be able to:
1. Recite CC Madhya 7.128 and tell the Kurma story as the chapter tells it (7.127-130).
2. Explain from the purports what "become a spiritual master" does and does not mean, and why the Lord told Kurma to stay at home.
3. State what the content of the instruction is ("the orders of Krishna as given in the Gita and Bhagavatam") and why that protects the speaker.
4. Apply NOI 5 and BG 3.21: speak to each person according to who he is, and let behaviour support the words.
5. Prepare the same topic for BSS or DPS from section R.

## E. Scriptural foundation

**CC Madhya 7.127** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/7/127/
> "Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu replied, 'Don't speak like that again. Better to remain at home and chant the holy name of Kṛṣṇa always.'"
- **Context:** The brahmana Kurma, in whose house the Lord took prasadam in South India, begged to leave home and travel with Him.
- **Purport point:** "It is not advisable in this Age of Kali to leave one's family suddenly, for people are not trained as proper brahmacaris and grhasthas." The Lord advised him to become purified at home by chanting "under the direction of a spiritual master", and "in the next verse… advises everyone to become an ideal householder by offenselessly chanting the Hare Krsna mantra and teaching the same principle to everyone he meets."
- **Use:** in the talk, as the setting. Residents should hear this clearly: the Lord's standard instruction was not "join Me" but "stay, chant, teach".

**CC Madhya 7.128** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/7/128/
> "Instruct everyone to follow the orders of Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa as they are given in the Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. In this way become a spiritual master and try to liberate everyone in this land."
- **Teaching:** Three parts: whom (everyone you meet), what (Krishna's orders as given in the Gita and Bhagavatam), result (you become a spiritual master by the Lord's order and liberate people).
- **Purport point:** "This is the sublime mission of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness." "Many people come and inquire whether they have to give up family life to join the Society, but that is not our mission." "We simply request everyone to chant the maha-mantra… If one is a little literate and can read Bhagavad-gita As It Is and Srimad-Bhagavatam, that is so much the better." The purport then lists the four sinful activities to give up, and says of illicit sex: "Out of these four items, illicit sex is very sinful. Every person must get married. Every woman especially must get married." It ends: "one should stay at home, chant the Hare Krsna mantra and preach the instructions of Krsna as they are given in the Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagavatam."
- **Application (preacher's):** The content is fixed (Krishna's orders), so the resident never has to invent a message or defend his own opinions; he repeats. That is what makes a junior resident a teacher without pretension.
- **Use:** in the talk; the central verse. Memorise the Bengali: *yare dekha, tare kaha 'krsna'-upadesa / amara ajnaya guru hana tara' ei desa*.

**CC Madhya 7.130** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/7/130/
> "At whosever house Śrī Caitanya accepted His alms by taking prasādam, He would convert the dwellers to His saṅkīrtana movement and advise them just as He advised the brāhmaṇa named Kūrma."
- **Purport point:** "One who surrenders to Him and is ready to follow Him with heart and soul does not need to change his location. Nor is it necessary for one to change his status. One may remain a householder, a medical practitioner, an engineer or whatever." "One should not try to be an artificially advanced devotee, thinking, 'I am a first-class devotee, so it is best not to accept any disciples.'" The purport also names the sahajiyas who decry the Gosvamis' and the acaryas' preaching as "another phase of materialism".
- **Application (preacher's):** Two opposite errors are closed off: refusing to teach ("I am too fallen") and refusing to teach ("I am too elevated"). The Lord's order covers both.
- **Use:** in the talk.

**NOI 5** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/noi/5/
- **Teaching:** Honour the devotee who chants within the mind; offer obeisances to the initiated devotee who worships the Deity; associate with and serve the pure devotee who is free of the propensity to criticise.
- **Purport point:** Rupa Gosvami "advises that we should meet with the Vaisnavas in an appropriate way, according to their particular status." One "should not remain a kanistha-adhikari… interested only in worshiping the Deity in the temple"; the madhyama "makes friends with the Lord's devotees, is merciful to the ignorant and avoids those who are envious by nature" (SB 11.2.46, quoted there).
- **Use:** in the talk, for *how* to speak to each person: friendship to devotees, mercy to the innocent, distance from the envious.

**BG 3.21** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/21/
> "Whatever action a great man performs, common men follow. And whatever standards he sets by exemplary acts, all the world pursues."
- **Purport point:** "A leader cannot teach the public to stop smoking if he himself smokes. Lord Caitanya said that a teacher should behave properly before he begins teaching."
- **Use:** in the talk: the resident's behaviour in the lift and at the gate is part of the instruction.

**CC Madhya 22.54** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/22/54/
> "The verdict of all revealed scriptures is that by even a moment's association with a pure devotee, one can attain all success."
- **Use:** in the talk: why a two-minute conversation matters; the purport notes that a *lava* is one eleventh of a second.

**BG 18.68, BG 18.69** — Verified (treated in trd-01). Here only one line: the promise to the one who explains the message.

## F. Philosophical explanation

1. **The order.** Lord Caitanya did not ask Kurma to leave home; He told him to stay, chant, and instruct whomever he met (CC Madhya 7.127-128). The purport says this is the Society's "sublime mission". Residents have left home, but the method they carry is the same one the Lord gave to a householder.
2. **Content, not personality.** The instruction's content is "the orders of Krishna as given in the Gita and the Bhagavatam". A resident does not teach his realisations; he repeats Krishna's. This is why a first-year brahmacari can be a teacher: the authority is in the message.
3. **"Become a spiritual master."** By the Lord's order (*amara ajnaya*), the repeater becomes guru for the one he instructs. The 7.130 purport removes the false modesty ("I am a first-class devotee, so it is best not to accept any disciples") and the sahajiya objection that preaching is materialism. The limit is also clear: the verse makes one a *siksa* (instructing) guru by repeating; it does not make one independent of one's own guru or the Society's standards.
4. **Behaviour is part of the teaching** (BG 3.21). The Lord "said that a teacher should behave properly before he begins teaching." The resident's dress, cleanliness, punctuality and dealings are watched by everyone he will later instruct.
5. **Levels** (NOI 5). The madhyama-adhikari deals with each person according to who he is: love for the Lord, friendship with devotees, mercy to the innocent, avoidance of the envious. "Whomever you meet" does not mean "say the same thing to everyone".
6. **Why small meetings matter** (CC Madhya 22.54). A moment's association with a pure devotee can give all success. The resident is not a pure devotee, but he carries the pure devotee's message, and the message is nondifferent from the Lord (see SB 1.2.17 purport, cited in dps-15).
7. **Misunderstandings.** (a) "Preaching means a lecture": the verse says instruct whomever you *meet*. (b) "I need a title or sannyasa": the Lord told a householder. (c) "If I preach I am proud": the 7.130 purport calls the refusal to accept and instruct people, under the cover of humility, an error to be avoided.

## H. Opening and engagement

**H.1 How to start (first 5 minutes).** Ask everyone to count silently how many people they spoke to yesterday who are
not devotees (vendors, guards, guests, parents, drivers, students). Collect three numbers aloud. Then say: "Lord Caitanya
gave one instruction about exactly those people. We will read it, see how He gave it to a man who wanted to leave home
and travel with Him, and work out what it asks of us tomorrow morning." Read CC Madhya 7.128 in Bengali.

**H.2 Opening questions**
1. "Of the people you counted, how many heard one sentence about Krishna from you? Be honest; no one has to say the number."
2. "What stops us from saying that sentence: lack of time, fear of being odd, or not knowing what to say?"
3. "Lord Caitanya told the brahmana Kurma to stay at home. Why do you think He did not take him along?"

## I. Core teaching points

1. **Stay, chant, instruct (CC Madhya 7.127-128).** The Lord refused Kurma's request to travel and gave him a mission at home. Example: a resident who thinks preaching will begin "when I am sent somewhere" has missed that the Lord's own standard instruction was for where the person already is. Transition: "What exactly is he to say?"
2. **Repeat Krishna's orders (7.128 purport).** The content is the Gita and Bhagavatam, the maha-mantra, and the four things to give up. Example: to the guard at the gate, one sentence: "Try chanting Hare Krishna while you stand here; it is from the Gita's own tradition." No invention needed. Transition: "But am I qualified to instruct anyone?"
3. **Two false modesties (7.130 purport).** "I am too fallen" and "I am too advanced to accept people" are both answered by the Lord's order. Example: a senior who stops taking new people because "I prefer my bhajana now" is addressed directly by this purport. Transition: "What makes the instruction believable?"
4. **Behaviour (BG 3.21).** The teacher behaves before he teaches. Example: the resident who instructs a student about the mind but is seen shouting at the cook has cancelled his own class. Transition: "And is it the same sentence to everyone?"
5. **Levels (NOI 5).** Friendship to devotees, mercy to the innocent, avoidance of the envious. Example: to a sincere guest, invite and explain; to a mocking one, a polite sentence and no argument. Transition: "Why do two minutes matter?"
6. **A moment's association (CC Madhya 22.54).** Krishna's message carries the pure devotee's power even in a short meeting; and the one who explains it is dearest to Krishna (BG 18.68-69). Transition to J.

## J. Stories and illustrations

**The brahmana Kurma (CC Madhya 7.121-131, as the text tells it).** On His South Indian tour the Lord stayed at the house
of a brahmana named Kurma, who served Him with great devotion. Kurma fell at His feet and begged to go with Him,
saying he could not bear separation. The Lord replied, "Don't speak like that again. Better to remain at home and chant
the holy name of Krsna always," and gave him the instruction of 7.128. Krishnadasa Kaviraja adds that at every house
where the Lord took prasadam He converted the family to sankirtana and advised them as He advised Kurma (7.130).
Devotional lesson: the Lord's mercy was to make Kurma a teacher where he stood.

**Srila Prabhupada's letter to his German disciples, 6 May 1977** (Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/letters/letter-to-german-disciples/).
Five months before his departure he quoted this very verse to them and wrote: "So our business is to satisfy the acaryas
and Krsna. If they are pleased then we know our work is successful. Go on spreading the sankirtana movement more and more.
I am only one person, but because all of you have kindly cooperated with me, this movement has now become a success all
over the world." Lesson: the founder's last instructions rested on CC Madhya 7.128; the residents carry it on.

## K. Anticipated questions and objections

1. **"The verse says 'become a spiritual master'. Can I initiate people?"** No. The verse makes the repeater an instructing guru for the one he instructs, by the Lord's order. Initiation follows the Society's standards and one's own guru. The 7.128 purport describes the content as chanting, reading the Gita and Bhagavatam, and giving up the four sinful activities; that is what we instruct.
2. **"The purport says 'every woman especially must get married' and allows a man more than one wife. Do I say that to a student?"** We present the purport as it is, including this, and explain the purpose Srila Prabhupada gives: "In that way there will be no prostitution in society." We do not add our own gloss, and we do not hide the sentence. Residents should know it is there and be able to explain the stated reason calmly.
3. **"I meet the same vendors every day. Won't they get tired of me?"** NOI 5 gives the measure: mercy to the innocent, not pressure. One honest sentence, prasadam, and respect, repeated over months, is the Kurma method; a sermon every morning is not.
4. **"I am new. The seniors preach; I serve."** The Lord told a householder with no title. The 7.130 purport answers both "too fallen" and "too elevated". Serving is right; one sentence to the person in front of you is also right.
5. **"What if I am asked a question I cannot answer?"** Say so, and bring the person to a senior or to the book. The content is Krishna's orders, not our cleverness.

## L. Case studies

**Case 1: The driver (Invented case for discussion).** Mohan, a resident, is driven to programmes three times a week by
the same hired driver, who has asked twice what the "Hare Krishna" people believe. Mohan has answered both times with
"it is a long story" and gone back to his phone.
- **Dilemma:** Is this a preaching opportunity or an intrusion?
- **Principles:** CC Madhya 7.128 (whomever you meet); NOI 5 (mercy to the innocent); CC Madhya 22.54 (a moment's association).
- **Questions:** What one sentence from the Gita could Mohan say? What could he give him? What would make the next drive different?
- **Analysis:** The driver has asked; the "innocent" of NOI 5 is in front of him. One sentence about the soul (BG 2.13), a small book or prasadam, and a sincere question about his family begin the Kurma method.
- **Takeaway:** The verse is fulfilled in the car, not only on the stage.

**Case 2: The senior who stopped (Invented case for discussion).** Govinda, twelve years in the ashram, now lectures
only to initiated devotees and says new people "disturb his bhajana"; juniors imitate him and the guest table is often
unattended.
- **Dilemma:** Mature focus, or the error the 7.130 purport names?
- **Principles:** CC Madhya 7.130 purport ("I am a first-class devotee, so it is best not to accept any disciples… Such thinking should be avoided"); BG 3.21 (what the senior does, the juniors follow).
- **Questions:** What is Govinda teaching the juniors by example? What would the Lord have said to him, judging from what He said to Kurma?
- **Takeaway:** Seniority increases the duty to instruct whomever one meets; it does not end it.

## M. Discussion and reflection

**M.1 Closing check questions**
1. What did Kurma ask for, and what did the Lord reply? (To travel with Him; "remain at home and chant… always": 7.127.)
2. What are we to instruct people in, according to 7.128? (Krishna's orders as given in the Gita and Bhagavatam.)
3. What two kinds of false modesty does the 7.130 purport reject? ("I am too fallen" and "I am a first-class devotee, so I should not accept anyone.")
4. What does BG 3.21 add to preaching? (The teacher's behaviour sets the standard others follow.)
5. How does the madhyama-adhikari deal with the innocent and with the envious? (Mercy; avoidance: NOI 5 purport, SB 11.2.46.)
6. Why does a short conversation matter? (A moment's association with a pure devotee can give all success: CC Madhya 22.54.)

**M.2 Activity (35 min): "A day of meetings."** In pairs. Each resident lists five people he will meet tomorrow who are
not devotees. For each, he writes one sentence of Krishna's instruction he could say (from the Gita or Bhagavatam, in
plain words) and one thing he could give (prasadam, a small book, an invitation). Partners check each sentence against
the verse: is it Krishna's order or my opinion? Then two role-plays of two minutes each (guard at the gate; parent of a
student), with the partner playing the person and giving one line of feedback.

**M.3 Reflection (private).** "Yesterday I met ___ people who are not devotees. Tomorrow I will say one sentence of
Krishna's instruction to ___ of them."

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary

On His tour of South India, Lord Caitanya stayed at the house of a brahmana named Kurma. Kurma begged to leave home
and travel with Him. The Lord said, "Don't speak like that again. Better to remain at home and chant the holy name of
Krsna always" (CC Madhya 7.127). Then He gave the instruction that Srila Prabhupada calls "the sublime mission" of our
Society: "Instruct everyone to follow the orders of Lord Sri Krsna as they are given in the Bhagavad-gita and
Srimad-Bhagavatam. In this way become a spiritual master and try to liberate everyone in this land" (CC Madhya 7.128).
He gave the same instruction at every house where He ate (7.130).

The instruction has three parts. Whom: everyone you meet. What: Krishna's own orders from the Gita and Bhagavatam, the
chanting of Hare Krishna, and giving up the four sinful activities. Result: by the Lord's order you become a teacher for
that person. Because the content is Krishna's, not ours, a new resident can do it as well as a senior. Srila Prabhupada
closes off two excuses: "I am too fallen to teach" and "I am too advanced to accept people" (7.130 purport).

Two things support the words. Our behaviour: "a teacher should behave properly before he begins teaching" (BG 3.21
purport). And the level of the person: the mature devotee is friendly with devotees, merciful to the innocent and avoids
the envious (NOI 5 purport). Even a short meeting matters, because a moment's association with a pure devotee's message
can give all success (CC Madhya 22.54), and Krishna promises that the one who explains His message is His dearest
servant (BG 18.68-69).

## S2. Key ideas

- Lord Caitanya's standard instruction: stay where you are, chant, and instruct whomever you meet (CC Madhya 7.127-128).
- The content is Krishna's orders from the Gita and Bhagavatam, so no one needs to invent a message.
- "Too fallen" and "too advanced" are both excuses the purport rejects (7.130).
- Behaviour teaches before words do (BG 3.21).
- Speak to each person according to who he is (NOI 5); even two minutes matter (CC Madhya 22.54).

## S3. Verses to remember

- **CC Madhya 7.128:** "Instruct everyone to follow the orders of Lord Sri Krsna as they are given in the Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagavatam. In this way become a spiritual master…" *For me:* I repeat Krishna's words to the person in front of me.
- **BG 3.21:** "Whatever action a great man performs, common men follow." *For me:* my conduct at the gate is part of my class.
- **CC Madhya 22.54:** "by even a moment's association with a pure devotee, one can attain all success." *For me:* a short conversation is not too small.

## S4. Words to know

- **Yare dekha, tare kaha krsna-upadesa:** "Whomever you meet, tell him Krishna's instruction" (CC Madhya 7.128).
- **Amara ajnaya guru hana:** "by My order become a spiritual master" (same verse).
- **Siksa-guru:** an instructing teacher; what the verse makes of the one who repeats Krishna's orders.
- **Kanistha / madhyama / uttama-adhikari:** beginner, intermediate and topmost devotee (NOI 5 purport).
- **Sahajiya:** one who takes the highest topics cheaply and decries preaching (7.130 purport).

## S5. Questions and answers

1. **Can I really be called a "spiritual master"?** By the Lord's order, for the person you instruct, yes, as an instructing teacher who repeats Krishna's orders. It does not make anyone an initiating guru or independent of his own guru.
2. **What exactly do I tell people?** Chant Hare Krishna; read the Gita and Bhagavatam; give up meat-eating, illicit sex, intoxication and gambling (7.128 purport). One sentence is enough to begin.
3. **I am new. Shouldn't I leave preaching to seniors?** The Lord told a householder with no position. "Too fallen" is one of the two excuses the 7.130 purport rejects.
4. **The same people every day: won't I annoy them?** Be merciful, not pushy (NOI 5): a sentence, prasadam and respect, repeated over months.
5. **What if I don't know the answer?** Say so and bring the person to a senior or to the book. The authority is Krishna's words, not ours.

**Answers:** 1. To travel with Him; "remain at home and chant the holy name… always." 2. Whomever you meet; Krishna's
orders from the Gita and Bhagavatam; you become a spiritual master by His order and liberate people. 3. "I am too fallen"

5. With mercy (NOI 5 purport). 6. A moment's association with a pure devotee can give all success.
