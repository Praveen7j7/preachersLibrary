## B. Why this topic belongs at this level

Builds on dps-30 (spontaneous devotional service introduced from NOD), trd-13 (why real taste grows by sadhana and service, not by
sahajiya shortcuts) and trd-33 (the rasas as Prabhupada presents them). Those pages treat the taste and its dangers. This page
gives the *doctrine*: the two kinds of sadhana-bhakti, their definitions, their relationship, and the exact words of Lord Caitanya
and Srila Prabhupada on how one passes from the first to the second.

TRD residents are asked about raganuga by sincere juniors and by visiting devotees of other lines; some are offered
"siddha-pranali" or told that ISKCON teaches "only vaidhi". A resident who knows only that "we follow the rules" cannot answer.
Lord Caitanya taught both kinds to Sanatana Gosvami in one sequence (CC Madhya 22.108–165), and Srila Prabhupada translated and
explained every verse. The answer is in his books; residents should be able to open them to the page.

## C. Central message

There are two sadhanas: regulative devotion for "those who have not attained the platform of spontaneous attachment" (CC Madhya
22.109), and raganuga, following the spontaneous love of Vrndavana's residents, which begins when "transcendental covetousness"
awakens from hearing about them (22.153–155). The same devotee does both at once: externally he "remains like a neophyte and
executes all the śāstric injunctions", internally, "in his original, purified, self-realized position", he serves in Vrndavana
(22.156–157). Srila Prabhupada's teaching is exact: the inner side belongs to the self-realised; the outer side never stops;
imitation is "mere imitation and has no value" (NOD 16).

## D. Learning outcomes

Participants should be able to:
1. Define vaidhi and raganuga bhakti in Lord Caitanya's words (CC Madhya 22.108–109, 22.149, 22.154) and ragatmika as their model
   (22.150–152).
2. State how raganuga begins: lobha (covetousness) awakened by hearing about the Vraja devotees (22.155; *Raga-vartma-candrika*
   1.5–6 as background), and what "does not care for the injunctions or reasonings of śāstra" does and does not mean (22.153
   purport).
3. Explain the two sides, external and internal (22.156–158), and Prabhupada's teaching that the external continues to the end
   (NOD 16; 22.109 purport).
4. Give the eligibility rule: freedom from anarthas (NOD 16), and answer "siddha-pranali" and imitation from NOD 16 and the
   22.153 purport.
5. Say how the regulative devotee relates to raganuga today: with faith, hearing and service, aspiring without claiming (22.152,
   22.164–165; CC Antya 20.28).

## E. Scriptural foundation

**CC Madhya 22.108–110** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/22/109/
> 22.108: "There are two processes of practical devotional service. One is regulative devotional service, and the other is spontaneous devotional service."
> 22.109: "Those who have not attained the platform of spontaneous attachment in devotional service render devotional service under the guidance of a bona fide spiritual master according to the regulative principles mentioned in the revealed scriptures. According to the revealed scriptures, this kind of devotional service is called vaidhī bhakti."
- **Context:** the Lord's teaching to Sanatana Gosvami on sadhana-bhakti; 22.110 quotes SB 2.1.5 as the injunction behind vaidhi
  (hear, glorify, remember the Lord "by one who wishes to become fearless").
- **Purport point (22.109):** "In the beginning, one has to hear from a bona fide spiritual master." "Devotional service must be
  without ulterior motives; then material conditions cannot check it. Gradually one can rise to the platform of spontaneous loving
  service." The two examples: "A child is sent to school by force to receive an education, but when he gets a little taste of
  education at an advanced age, he automatically participates and becomes a learned scholar." "One has to learn to use a typewriter
  by following the regulative principles of the typing book." And the conclusion: "Dormant love for Kṛṣṇa exists in everyone's
  heart, and it simply has to be awakened by the regulative process of devotional service." "Spontaneous service is not
  artificial. One simply has to come to that platform by rendering devotional service according to the regulative principles."
- **Use:** in the talk, as the definition and as Prabhupada's whole teaching in one purport.

**CC Madhya 22.149–152** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/22/149/
> 22.149: "The original inhabitants of Vṛndāvana are attached to Kṛṣṇa spontaneously in devotional service. Nothing can compare to such spontaneous devotional service, which is called rāgātmikā bhakti. When a devotee follows in the footsteps of the devotees of Vṛndāvana, his devotional service is called rāgānugā bhakti."
> 22.151: "The primary characteristic of spontaneous love is deep attachment for the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Absorption in thought of Him is a marginal characteristic."
> 22.152: "Thus devotional service which consists of rāga [deep attachment] is called rāgātmikā, spontaneous loving service. If a devotee covets such a position, he is considered to be most fortunate."
- **Teaching:** ragatmika is the Vrajavasis' own love (22.150 = BRS 1.2.272); raganuga is *following* it. Coveting it is fortune,
  not fault (22.152). trd-13 uses 22.149 for taste; here it is the definition.
- **Use:** in the talk.

**CC Madhya 22.153–155** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/22/153/
> 22.153: "If one follows in the footsteps of the inhabitants of Vṛndāvana out of such transcendental covetousness, he does not care for the injunctions or reasonings of śāstra. That is the way of spontaneous love."
> 22.154 (BRS 1.2.270): "Devotional service in spontaneous love is vividly expressed and manifested by the inhabitants of Vṛndāvana. Devotional service that accords with their devotional service is called rāgānugā bhakti, or devotional service following in the wake of spontaneous loving service."
> 22.155 (BRS 1.2.292): "When an advanced, realized devotee hears about the affairs of the devotees of Vṛndāvana — in the mellows of śānta, dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya and mādhurya — he becomes inclined in one of these ways, and his intelligence becomes attracted. Indeed, he begins to covet that particular type of devotion."
- **Purport point (22.153):** Bhaktisiddhanta: the attraction is "technically called svarūpa-upalabdhi. This stage is not achieved
  in the beginning. In the beginning one has to render service strictly according to the regulative principles set forth by the
  revealed scriptures and the spiritual master. By continuously rendering service through the process of vaidhī bhakti, one's
  natural inclination is gradually awakened." On "does not care for sastra": "An advanced devotee situated on the platform of
  spontaneity is already very expert in śāstric instruction, logic and argument. When he comes to the point of eternal love for
  Kṛṣṇa, no one can deviate him from that position, neither by argument nor by śāstric evidence." And: "Unless one follows the six
  Gosvāmīs ... one cannot be a bona fide spontaneous lover of Kṛṣṇa." The sahajiyas "manufacture their own way" and "are not equal
  to advanced devotees (paramahaṁsas). Debauchees and paramahaṁsas are not on the same level."
- **Teaching:** the beginning of raganuga is lobha from hearing (22.155), in "an advanced, realized devotee". Independence from
  sastra means he cannot be argued out of his love, not that he drops the rules.
- **Use:** in the talk; 22.153 purport read in full.

**CC Madhya 22.156–161** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/22/156-157/
> 22.156–157: "There are two processes by which one may execute this rāgānugā bhakti — external and internal. When self-realized, the advanced devotee externally remains like a neophyte and executes all the śāstric injunctions, especially those concerning hearing and chanting. But within his mind, in his original, purified, self-realized position, he serves Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana in his particular way. He serves Kṛṣṇa twenty-four hours a day, all day and night."
> 22.158 (BRS 1.2.295): "The advanced devotee who is inclined to spontaneous loving service should follow the activities of a particular associate of Kṛṣṇa's in Vṛndāvana. He should execute service externally as a regulative devotee as well as internally from his self-realized position."
> 22.159: "Actually the inhabitants of Vṛndāvana are very dear to Kṛṣṇa. If one wants to engage in spontaneous loving service, he must follow the inhabitants of Vṛndāvana and constantly engage in devotional service within his mind."
> 22.160 (BRS 1.2.294): "The devotee should always think of Kṛṣṇa within himself and should choose a very dear devotee who is a servitor of Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana. One should constantly engage in topics about that servitor and his loving relationship with Kṛṣṇa, and one should live in Vṛndāvana. If one is physically unable to go to Vṛndāvana, he should mentally live there."
> 22.161: "Kṛṣṇa has many types of devotees — some are servants, some are friends, some are parents, and some are conjugal lovers. Devotees who are situated in one of these attitudes of spontaneous love according to their choice are considered to be on the path of spontaneous loving service."
- **Teaching:** the words "When self-realized" (22.156) and "from his self-realized position" (22.158) fix who performs the inner
  side. The outer side is "all the śāstric injunctions, especially those concerning hearing and chanting."
- **Use:** in the talk, read slowly; this is the page's centre.

**CC Madhya 22.162–165** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/22/165/
> 22.164: "If one engages in spontaneous loving service to the Lord, his affection for the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa gradually increases."
> 22.165: "In the seed of affection, there is attachment which goes by two names, rati and bhāva. The Supreme Personality of Godhead comes under the control of such attachment."
- **Teaching:** 22.162 (SB 3.25.38) and 22.163 (BRS 1.2.308) honour those who love the Lord as son, friend, husband; the fruit is

- **Purport point (22.165):** Bhaktisiddhanta: externally the devotee performs the nine items beginning with hearing and chanting;

**NOD 15, 16** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/nod/16/
- **NOD 15 (Spontaneous Devotional Service):** the Vrndavana associates "don't have to learn anything about devotional service";
  Rupa Gosvami defines raganuga as "spontaneous attraction for something while completely absorbed in thoughts of it, with an
  intense desire of love"; the two categories, "sensual attraction" and "relationship"; the gopis and the Yadus as spontaneous,
  Kamsa and Sisupala "not accepted as devotional service" because unfavourable.
- **NOD 16 (Spontaneous Devotion Further Described), "Eligibility for spontaneous devotional service":** "such eagerness to follow in
  the footsteps of the denizens of Vraja (Vṛndāvana) is not possible unless one is freed from material contamination." "Sometimes
  someone is found imitating such devotional love, but factually he is not freed from anarthas, or unwanted habits. It has been
  seen that a so-called devotee proclaims himself a follower of Nanda, Yaśodā or the gopīs, while at the same time his abominable
  attraction for mundane sex life is visible. Such a manifestation of divine love is mere imitation and has no value." "Therefore,
  in the beginning, everyone should strictly follow the regulative principles of devotional service according to the injunctions
  of the scriptures and the spiritual master. Only after the stage of liberation from material contamination can one actually
  aspire to follow in the footsteps of the devotees in Vṛndāvana." On siddha-pranali: "we should be careful about the so-called
  siddha-praṇālī. The siddha-praṇālī process is followed by a class of men who are not very authorized and who have manufactured
  their own way of devotional service. They imagine that they have become associates of the Lord simply by thinking of themselves
  like that." "In the opinion of Rūpa Gosvāmī, such activities are simply disturbances to the standard way of devotional service."
  And: "Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī says that learned ācāryas recommend that we follow the regulative principles even after the development
  of spontaneous love for Kṛṣṇa." On conjugal love: it "can be possible only with those who are already engaged in following the
  regulative principles of devotional service, specifically in the worship of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in the temple."
- **Use:** in the talk; NOD 16 is Prabhupada's fullest statement of eligibility and of the imitation danger.

## F. Philosophical explanation

1. **Two sadhanas, one goal.** Sadhana-bhakti is practice that awakens dormant love. Where the impulse is the scripture's order,
   it is vaidhi; where the impulse is attachment itself, following the Vrajavasis, it is raganuga (CC Madhya 22.108–109, 22.149;
   *Raga-vartma-candrika* 1.3 says the same). Both are sadhana; neither is prema yet.
2. **Ragatmika is the model, raganuga the following.** The Vrajavasis' love is ragatmika, marked by deep attachment and absorption
   (22.150–151). It cannot be practised; it can be followed. Raganuga is "devotional service following in the wake" of it (22.154).
3. **How raganuga begins: lobha from hearing.** An "advanced, realized devotee hears about the affairs of the devotees of Vṛndāvana"
   and "begins to covet that particular type of devotion" (22.155). Visvanatha: greed comes by the Lord's mercy or a devotee's
   (RVC 1.6). So the preparation for raganuga is the vaidhi practice itself: hearing with attention, by which Krishna cleanses the
   heart (SB 1.2.17), and the association of advanced devotees. No one *decides* to be raganuga.
4. **Not caring for sastra means he cannot be argued out of love.** Prabhupada's purport (22.153): the raganuga devotee "is
   already very expert in śāstric instruction, logic and argument"; no one can deviate him "neither by argument nor by śāstric
   evidence". Visvanatha adds the cow: even the greedy man must learn from one who knows how to get the milk (RVC 1.6). The
   phrase never licenses dropping the rules.
5. **Two sides, and who does the inner one.** "When self-realized, the advanced devotee externally remains like a neophyte and
   executes all the śāstric injunctions" while "within his mind, in his original, purified, self-realized position, he serves
   Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana" (22.156–157). Prabhupada: the pure devotee "should always continue to act as a neophyte devotee as long as
   his material body is there" (NOD 16). The external practice is lifelong; the internal service is for the self-realised.
6. **Eligibility: freedom from anarthas.** "Such eagerness ... is not possible unless one is freed from material contamination"
   (NOD 16). The one who claims the gopis' following while "his abominable attraction for mundane sex life is visible" shows "mere
   imitation" (NOD 16). Bhaktisiddhanta's hundred warnings (trd-39) say the same in a hundred ways.
7. **Siddha-pranali and manufactured ways.** Prabhupada names the "so-called siddha-praṇālī" of men "who have manufactured their
   own way", who "imagine that they have become associates of the Lord simply by thinking of themselves like that", and calls it,
   with Rupa Gosvami, a disturbance (NOD 16). The genuine inner service comes from realisation under the six Gosvamis (22.153
   purport), not from a formula.
8. **How we relate to raganuga now.** With faith that the dormant love is there and "simply has to be awakened by the regulative
   process" (22.109 purport); with hearing about the Vrajavasis from Prabhupada's books; with the aspiration 22.152 calls fortune;
   without the claim that 20.28 forbids. The Lord gave Raghunatha both sides in one sentence (CC Antya 6.237): chant always, and
   serve within the mind. The chanting is ours today; the inner service comes as the name purifies.
9. **Misunderstandings.**
   - *"ISKCON teaches only vaidhi."* Prabhupada translated and taught every raganuga verse here and devoted two NOD chapters to it.
     He taught the eligibility too.
   - *"Raganuga means I may neglect the morning programme."* The raganuga devotee "executes all the śāstric injunctions, especially
     those concerning hearing and chanting" (22.156–157).
   - *"I must be given my siddha-deha by a guru to progress."* The inner identity is realised (svarupa-upalabdhi, 22.153 purport),
     not assigned; the imitation of assignment is what NOD 16 warns against.
   - *"Hearing about the gopis is forbidden in ISKCON."* Hearing from Prabhupada's books and the acharyas under qualified
     guidance is the very cause of lobha (22.155); what is forbidden is imitation and premature public discussion (trd-33, trd-44).
10. **Pitch for TRD.** Give the verses exactly, with the words "when self-realized" and "freed from material contamination"
    underlined. Residents leave knowing the doctrine, the eligibility and the danger, and with their regulative practice
    strengthened, not weakened.

## I. Core teaching points

1. **Two sadhanas** (22.108–109): rule-prompted and attachment-prompted; both awaken dormant love (22.109 purport). *Example:* the
   child forced to school who later studies by taste. *Transition:* what is the attachment following?
2. **Ragatmika is the Vrajavasis' love; raganuga follows it** (22.149–154; NOD 15). *Transition:* how does following begin?
3. **Lobha from hearing, in an advanced devotee** (22.155; SB 1.2.17; RVC 1.6 background). *Transition:* and then?
4. **Two sides at once: external like a neophyte, internal when self-realised** (22.156–161; CC Antya 6.237). *Transition:* who is
   eligible for the inner side?
5. **Only one freed from anarthas; imitation has no value; siddha-pranali is manufactured** (NOD 16; 22.153 purport). *Transition:*
   the fruit for the genuine.
6. **Rati and bhava, which bring Krishna under control** (22.164–165). *Transition:* and us?
7. **Faith, hearing, service, aspiration without claim** (22.109 purport; 22.152; CC Antya 20.28).

## J. Stories and illustrations

**Lord Caitanya teaches Sanatana Gosvami** (CC Madhya 22, within the Sanatana-siksa of Madhya 20–24). Sanatana, who left the
Nawab's service to come to the Lord, is taught the whole of sadhana-bhakti at Benares: its sixty-four items, then its two kinds,
then raganuga, in the verses above. *Devotional lesson:* the Lord entrusted the full doctrine, including raganuga, to the devotee
He would send to Vrndavana to write the books and establish the standards (Hari-bhakti-vilasa; trd-34); doctrine and standards
came from the same mouth to the same man.

**The Lord's two-sided instruction to Raghunatha dasa** (CC Antya 6.236–237). To the young Gosvami who had just reached Him in
Puri, the Lord gives the external side (no worldly talk, no fine food or dress, no expecting honour, all respect to others, always
chant) and the internal side ("within your mind render service to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana") in two verses. *Devotional lesson:*
the two sides of 22.156–157 given to one person; Raghunatha's later life at Radha-kunda shows the inner side held together with the
strictest outer practice.

**Prabhupada's examples** (22.109 purport): the child sent to school by force who later becomes a scholar by taste; the typist who
follows the typing book and then types "without even looking at the keys". *Devotional lesson:* spontaneity is the fruit of the
rule, not its enemy.

## K. Anticipated questions and objections

1. **"Does ISKCON teach raganuga at all?"** Srila Prabhupada translated and explained every raganuga verse of CC Madhya 22 and gave
   it two chapters of NOD. He also taught its eligibility (NOD 16) and its danger. We teach exactly that.
2. **"22.153 says the raganuga devotee does not care for sastra. So the rules end?"** No. The purport: he "is already very expert in
   śāstric instruction" and cannot be deviated from his love by argument; and 22.156–157: externally he "executes all the śāstric
   injunctions". Visvanatha's cow example (RVC 1.6) says the same: even the greedy must learn the method.
3. **"A visiting devotee offers siddha-pranali. Should I take it?"** Prabhupada: "we should be careful about the so-called
   siddha-praṇālī", followed by men who "have manufactured their own way" and "imagine that they have become associates of the Lord
   simply by thinking of themselves like that" (NOD 16). The inner identity is realised as the heart is purified (svarupa-upalabdhi,
   22.153 purport). Decline, and tell your authorities.
4. **"How will I know when lobha has come?"** Not by deciding. 22.155 places it in "an advanced, realized devotee"; NOD 16 places it
   after freedom from anarthas; CC Antya 20.28 says the one who has love does not think himself a devotee. Keep hearing and
   serving; leave the assessment to guru and senior Vaisnavas.
5. **"Then may I never think of Vraja while chanting?"** The Lord told Raghunatha to chant always and serve Radha-Krishna within the
   mind (Antya 6.237). Hear Prabhupada's Krsna book and SB Canto 10; let the mind rest where the hearing puts it. Do not assign
   yourself an identity or a service; that is the manufactured way.
6. **"Which rasa should I aim for?"** 22.161 says devotees are situated in one "according to their choice", and 22.155 says the
   choice arises from hearing, as the intelligence "becomes attracted". The rasa is discovered in the heart under guidance, not
   picked from a list (trd-33, trd-41).
7. **"Isn't aspiring to raganuga pride?"** 22.152: "If a devotee covets such a position, he is considered to be most fortunate."
   Aspiring is fortune; claiming is the sahajiya's pride (20.28 purport).
8. **"Why is this page sensitive?"** Because the inner side and the Vraja associates are easily misused, and because the head reviews
   every rasa page. The content is Prabhupada's; the care is ours.

## L. Case studies

*Invented cases for discussion. Not real incidents.*

**Case 1: The early riser who stopped rising.** Nitai dasa, eight years in the ashram, read a book on raganuga from another line
and now says the morning programme is "for vaidhi devotees"; he chants in his room and reads about Radha-kunda.
- **Dilemma:** has he advanced, or left the path?
- **Principles:** 22.156–157 (externally like a neophyte, "especially those concerning hearing and chanting"); NOD 16 ("we follow the
  regulative principles even after the development of spontaneous love"); 22.153 purport (following the six Gosvamis).
- **Questions:** Which single verse answers him? What has he confused "does not care for sastra" with? Who should speak to him, and
  how, so that he hears it as care?
- **Takeaway:** the inner side never cancels the outer; dropping the outer is the surest sign the inner is imitation.

**Case 2: The attracted hearer.** Vrinda devi dasi, a congregation member of twenty years, finds that hearing Krsna book's Vraja

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary

Lord Caitanya taught Sanatana Gosvami that practical devotional service has two kinds (CC Madhya 22.108). Those "who have not
attained the platform of spontaneous attachment" serve under a spiritual master according to the scriptures' regulations; this is
vaidhi bhakti (22.109). Srila Prabhupada explains it with two examples: a child is sent to school by force and later studies by
taste; a typist follows the typing book and later types without looking at the keys. "Dormant love for Kṛṣṇa exists in everyone's
heart, and it simply has to be awakened by the regulative process of devotional service" (22.109 purport).

The inhabitants of Vrndavana love Krishna spontaneously; that love is ragatmika, marked by deep attachment and absorption in Him
(22.149–151). When a devotee follows in their footsteps, his service is raganuga (22.149, 22.154). Coveting such a position makes
one "most fortunate" (22.152). Raganuga begins when "an advanced, realized devotee hears about the affairs of the devotees of
Vṛndāvana" and "begins to covet that particular type of devotion" (22.155). Such a devotee "does not care for the injunctions or
reasonings of śāstra" (22.153), which Prabhupada explains to mean that he is already expert in scripture and no argument can move
him from his love, not that he drops the rules. Raganuga has two sides: "When self-realized, the advanced devotee externally
remains like a neophyte and executes all the śāstric injunctions, especially those concerning hearing and chanting. But within his
mind, in his original, purified, self-realized position, he serves Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana" (22.156–157). He follows a particular
associate of Krishna and lives in Vrndavana, at least in mind (22.158–160). The fruit is rati and bhava, which bring Krishna
under control (22.164–165). The Lord gave both sides to Raghunatha dasa Gosvami in one sentence: always chant, "and within your
mind render service to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana" (CC Antya 6.237).

Prabhupada gives the eligibility plainly: eagerness to follow the Vrajavasis "is not possible unless one is freed from material
contamination" (NOD 16). One who claims to follow the gopis while his attraction to sex life is visible shows "mere imitation"
that "has no value". The "so-called siddha-pranali" of men who "have manufactured their own way" and "imagine that they have become
associates of the Lord simply by thinking of themselves like that" is, in Rupa Gosvami's opinion, a disturbance. Learned acharyas
"recommend that we follow the regulative principles even after the development of spontaneous love" (NOD 16). So we practise with
faith that the love is already in the heart, hear about the Vrajavasis from Prabhupada's books, aspire without claiming (the real
devotee "does not think himself a devotee", CC Antya 20.28), and leave the assessment of our stage to guru and senior Vaisnavas.

## S2. Key ideas

- Two sadhanas: vaidhi, prompted by scripture's order; raganuga, prompted by attachment following the Vrajavasis (22.108–109,
  22.149).
- Ragatmika is the Vrajavasis' own love; raganuga follows it (22.149–154).
- Raganuga begins with lobha from hearing, in an advanced devotee (22.155).
- Two sides: externally like a neophyte, all injunctions; internally, "when self-realized", service in Vrndavana (22.156–158).
- Eligibility is freedom from anarthas; imitation has no value; siddha-pranali is manufactured (NOD 16).
- We practise with faith that the love is already there (22.109 purport), aspiring without claiming (22.152; Antya 20.28).

## S3. Verses to remember

- **CC Madhya 22.109 purport:** "Dormant love for Kṛṣṇa exists in everyone's heart, and it simply has to be awakened by the
  regulative process of devotional service." *For me:* my morning programme is awakening love, not replacing it.
- **CC Madhya 22.156–157:** "When self-realized, the advanced devotee externally remains like a neophyte and executes all the
  śāstric injunctions, especially those concerning hearing and chanting." *For me:* the outer side never ends.
- **CC Madhya 22.152:** "If a devotee covets such a position, he is considered to be most fortunate." *For me:* I may aspire.
- **CC Antya 6.237:** "Always chant the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa, and within your mind render service to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in
  Vṛndāvana." *For me:* the Lord's own two-sided instruction.

## S4. Words to know

- **Sadhana-bhakti:** devotional service in practice, which awakens dormant love.
- **Vaidhi bhakti:** practice prompted by the scriptures' orders, under a spiritual master.
- **Ragatmika bhakti:** the spontaneous love of Krishna's eternal associates in Vrndavana.
- **Raganuga bhakti:** practice that follows (anuga) that spontaneous love (raga).
- **Lobha:** covetousness or sacred greed for the Vrajavasis' devotion, awakened by hearing.
- **Svarupa-upalabdhi:** realisation of one's own spiritual identity; "not achieved in the beginning" (22.153 purport).
- **Siddha-pranali:** a so-called method by which a guru assigns a spiritual identity; Prabhupada calls it manufactured (NOD 16).

## S5. Questions and answers

1. **Does ISKCON teach only vaidhi bhakti?** No. Srila Prabhupada translated and explained every raganuga verse in CC Madhya 22 and
   gave it two chapters of NOD; he also taught who is eligible and warned against imitation.
2. **If the raganuga devotee "does not care for sastra", do the rules end?** No. Prabhupada's purport says he is already expert in
   scripture and cannot be argued out of his love; and 22.156–157 says he externally follows all the injunctions.
3. **Someone offers me siddha-pranali. Should I accept?** Prabhupada: "we should be careful about the so-called siddha-praṇālī",
   followed by unauthorised men who "have manufactured their own way" (NOD 16). Decline, and speak to your authorities.
4. **How will I know if I have lobha?** Not by deciding. It comes to "an advanced, realized devotee" (22.155) who is "freed from
   material contamination" (NOD 16), and such a devotee "does not think himself a devotee" (Antya 20.28). Keep hearing and
   serving; let guru and senior devotees assess.
5. **May I think of Vrndavana while chanting?** The Lord told Raghunatha to chant always and serve Radha-Krishna within the mind
   (Antya 6.237). Hear Prabhupada's Krsna book and let the mind rest where the hearing puts it; do not assign yourself an identity.
6. **Is wanting raganuga pride?** Wanting it is fortune (22.152); claiming it is the imitator's pride (Antya 20.28 purport).
