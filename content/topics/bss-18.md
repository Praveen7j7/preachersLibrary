# 3. "Brahmacari" is Also a Designation

> BSS (brahmacari training) · 120 min · drafted, not yet reviewed · sensitive (celibacy, status): respectful, no shame.
> Based on chapter 3 of *Brahmacarya in Krsna Consciousness* (BIKC), Bhakti Vikasa Swami: summarised and cited, quoted briefly.

## A. Topic overview
| | |

## B. Why this topic belongs at this level
Having praised the vow (bss-16, 17), BIKC protects the student from its shadow: pride in status. Humility keeps
the vow Krsna conscious.

| Level | Treatment |
|---|---|
| VL1 | "Who am I?": body versus self, with BG 5.18 only. |
| VL2 | Varna and ashrama by quality (BG 4.13). |

Not done: the technical rules of the ashrama or its dangers.

## C. Central message
Even "brahmacari" is a designation; the soul is Krsna's eternal servant (CC Madhya 20.108), so we keep the
vow as a means and measure ourselves only by the quality of our devotion.

## D. Learning outcomes
1. Quote and explain Lord Caitanya's self-description (CC Madhya 13.80).
2. State why pure devotion means freedom from all designations (CC Madhya 19.170).
3. Explain why varna and ashrama do not decide a Vaishnava's advancement (CC Antya 4.66-67).
4. Explain why brahmacarya is still emphasised for us now (BIKC; NOI 1).

## E. Scriptural foundation

**CC Madhya 13.80** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/13/80/
> "I am not a brāhmaṇa, I am not a kṣatriya, I am not a vaiśya or a śūdra. Nor am I a brahmacārī, a householder, a vānaprastha or a sannyāsī."
- Context: Lord Caitanya declares His identity.
- Teaching: He denies all eight social and ashrama designations and identifies as the servant of the servant of Krsna.
- Application (preacher's): A brahmacari can wear the title lightly because the Lord Himself does not claim any.
- Use: in the talk.

**CC Madhya 19.170** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/19/170/
> "…one is freed from all material designations, and one's senses are purified simply by being employed in the service of the Lord."
- Context: Quoted from the Narada-pancaratra.
- Teaching: Bhakti is service of the master of the senses; freedom from designations is a result.
- Application (preacher's): BIKC cites this verse's idea; use as the definition.
- Use: in the talk.

**CC Madhya 20.108** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/20/108-109/
> "It is the living entity's constitutional position to be an eternal servant of Kṛṣṇa…"
- Teaching: Eternal servant, the identity beneath all designations.
- Use: in the talk.

**CC Antya 4.66-67** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/4/66/
> "A person born in a low family is not unfit for discharging devotional service to Lord Kṛṣṇa, nor is one fit for devotional service simply because he is born in an aristocratic family of brāhmaṇas."
- Teaching: Birth status does not decide fitness for devotional service.
- Application (preacher's): If birth does not decide it, neither does an ashrama title.
- Use: in the talk.

**SB 5.12.12** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/5/12/12/
> "…One cannot realize the Absolute Truth simply by observing celibacy [brahmacarya]…"
- Context: Jada Bharata to King Rahugana.
- Teaching: Celibacy and the other external disciplines do not alone give realisation; the dust of the feet of great devotees is needed.
- Application (preacher's): Celibacy is necessary and not sufficient; humble association completes it.
- Use: in the talk.

**CC Madhya 8.128** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/madhya/8/128/
> "Whether one is a brāhmaṇa, a sannyāsī or a śūdra — regardless of what he is — he can become a spiritual master if he knows the science of Kṛṣṇa."
- Teaching: Knowledge of Krsna, not status, qualifies.
- Use: extension.

**BG 4.13 and SB 7.11.35** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/13/ ; https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/7/11/35/
> "…the four divisions of human society are created by Me…" (BG 4.13) · "…he should be accepted according to those symptoms of classification." (SB 7.11.35)
- Teaching: The system is by quality and work; its role is service.
- Use: extension.

**BG 5.18** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/5/18/
> "The humble sages, by virtue of true knowledge, see with equal vision a learned and gentle brāhmaṇa, a cow, an elephant, a dog and a dog-eater."
- Teaching: Equal vision from true knowledge.
- Use: in the talk (one line).

**NOI 1** — Verified · https://vedabase.io/en/library/noi/1/
> "A sober person who can tolerate the urge to speak, the mind's demands…"
- Context: BIKC quotes the purport on go-dasa (servant of the senses) versus gosvami.
- Teaching: Prabhupada's purport says one who is not master of the senses is called go-dasa, not gosvami (BIKC quotes it; read the purport).
- Application (preacher's): This is why we still emphasise the vow.
- Use: in the talk.

## F. Philosophical explanation
**Principle:** the soul is not the body, so every body-based title (including brahmacari) is a designation (upadhi).
**Why it matters:** pride in status blocks bhakti as surely as lust does. **BIKC points (summarised):** do not stress
distinctions between varnas and ashramas, especially among devotees; Mayavadi sannyasis may keep stricter rules than
many devotees but are not dear to Krsna; Lord Caitanya respected them socially but associated with devotees; His
closest associates included householders, an old woman and a renunciate who did not take all formalities, and He
blessed Haridasa Thakura as acarya of the holy name. (BIKC; Requires source review against CC Adi 7, Madhya 25 and the
Antya chapters before telling any of these as a story.) **Both/and:** for a neophyte the vow must be emphasised, since
"unless we make a rigid program to control our senses, there will never be any possibility of advancement" (BIKC).
**Misunderstandings:** (1) "Designations don't matter, so rules don't" -- the opposite. (2) "Gentler on sannyasis
means lax" -- no. (3) "Humility means doubt" -- no, it is service.
**Pitch:** warm and humble; make the room lighter, not looser.

## H. Opening and engagement
**H.1** Ask: "Introduce yourself to your neighbour without saying your name, job, family or title: only who you are." After
one minute, ask how it felt. Say: "The Lord answered this question in one verse." Read CC Madhya 13.80.
**H.2** (1) What titles do people use for you? (2) Which one do you protect most? (3) Whose service would you like to be known for?

## I. Core teaching points
1. **The Lord disclaims all designations (CC Madhya 13.80).**
2. **Bhakti frees from designations (CC Madhya 19.170).**
3. **Eternal servant is the identity (CC Madhya 20.108).**
4. **Status does not decide devotion (CC Antya 4.66-67; SB 5.12.12).**
5. **For us, the vow is still necessary (NOI 1; BIKC).**
6. **Respect everyone; associate with devotees (BIKC on Lord Caitanya's conduct).**

## J. Stories and illustrations
BIKC retells how Lord Caitanya, in Varanasi, avoided the Mayavadi sannyasis and lived among householder devotees; they criticised
Him and later were converted (BIKC, citing CC Adi ch. 7 and Madhya ch. 25). **Requires source review** against those chapters before
telling it with details. Devotional lesson: devotion and association decide closeness to the Lord.
Illustration (preacher's): a uniform tells others a role; a servant of Krsna is known by service. End: serve.

## K. Anticipated questions and objections
1. *If it's a designation, why keep it?* BIKC: in our present position the program of sense control is essential.
2. *Am I better than a householder?* CC Antya 4.66-67: status does not decide. Lord Caitanya had householder associates.
3. *Does this lower the rules?* No; it deepens the motive.
4. *What if I feel proud or ashamed?* Both are designation-thinking. Speak to a senior; no shame. Tele-MANAS 14416 (India) or local support if distress is severe.
5. *What about nationality or caste titles?* Chaitanya Charan Das's article on designations and duty to country (O) gives a supporting view; principles from CC Madhya 19.170.

## L. Case studies
**Invented case for discussion.** Aman, a brahmacari, looks down on a married devotee who preaches very effectively.
- Principles: CC Antya 4.66-67; BG 5.18.
- Questions: What is Aman missing? What can he learn?
- Analysis: Ask the householder for association and hear; offer service.
- Takeaway: Quality of devotion measures advancement.

## M. Discussion and reflection
**M.1** (1) Whose servant does the Lord call Himself? *Servant of the servant of the servant of Krsna, maintainer of the gopis.* (2) What does bhakti free us from? *Designations (19.170).* (3) Does celibacy alone give realisation? *No (SB 5.12.12).* (4) Why keep the vow? *Sense control is essential for us now.* (5) What decides advancement? *Quality of devotion.*
**M.2** *Introduce without titles (10 min).* *Designation list (15 min):* groups list all titles they carry and mark which are service.
**M.3** Private: "Which title do I need to hold more lightly?"

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary
Lord Caitanya said He is not a brahmana, kshatriya, vaishya or shudra, and not a brahmacari, householder, vanaprastha or
sannyasi, but the servant of the servant of Krsna (CC Madhya 13.80). Pure devotion is freedom from all designations
(CC Madhya 19.170). So "brahmacari" is also a designation, and the goal is to be a pure devotee. Birth or status does not
decide fitness (CC Antya 4.66-67), and celibacy alone does not give realisation (SB 5.12.12). Still, for us in the
present stage, a strict program of sense control is necessary (BIKC). We hold the vow seriously and the title lightly.

## S2. Key ideas
- Identity: eternal servant of Krsna.
- Titles are designations.
- The vow is a means.
- Devotion decides advancement.

## S3. Verses to remember
- **CC Madhya 13.80** -- the Lord: servant of the servant of Krsna.
- **CC Madhya 20.108** -- constitutional position: eternal servant.

## S4. Words to know
- *Upadhi* -- designation. · *Varna* -- social division. · *Ashrama* -- stage of life.

## S5. Questions and answers
1. *If titles don't matter, why keep rules?* We need sense control now.
2. *Is a brahmacari higher than a householder?* Not by status; devotion measures.
3. *What if I feel proud?* Hold the title lightly and serve.
4. *Does celibacy give realisation?* Not by itself (SB 5.12.12).

## S6. Check your understanding
1. Who does the Lord say He is? 2. What does bhakti free us from? 3. Does birth decide fitness for service? 4. Is the vow still needed?

**Answers:** 1 Servant of the servant of Krsna (CC Madhya 13.80). 2 Designations (19.170). 3 No (Antya 4.66-67). 4 Yes, for the present stage (BIKC).
