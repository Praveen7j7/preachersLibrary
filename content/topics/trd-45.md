# Srimati Radharani: the Lord's pleasure potency (hladini-sakti)

> **TRD · up to 180 min · review: drafted · `sensitive: true` (Radha-tattva; madhurya-rasa; the head reviews).** Group G, page 5. Who is
> Srimati Radharani? Srila Prabhupada answers in the purports to Caitanya-caritamrta Adi 4.55–100, where Krishnadasa Kaviraja explains the
> fifth of Svarupa Damodara's fourteen verses: She is Krishna's own internal potency in its pleasure-giving aspect (hladini), one with Him
> as heat with fire and yet eternally two "to enjoy rasa" (4.57); the embodiment of mahabhava, the summit of love (4.68–69); the source of
> every consort and every goddess of fortune (4.76–81); "the greatest servitor" (4.87 purport), through whom the living entities' service
> reaches Krishna (4.56 purport); and the One whose mood and complexion the Lord took to appear as Sri Caitanya (4.99–100). The page is
> theology, not pastime: it gives the resident the siddhanta in Prabhupada's words and his reasons for seeking Her mercy. **Source note:**
> Brahma-samhita 5.37 is quoted in Prabhupada's CC translation (Adi 4.72) and from the BBT Brahma-samhita with Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's
> purport. Ujjvala-nilamani (Radha-prakarana) is cited only as Prabhupada quotes it (Adi 4.70; Madhya 8.161); the file is background
> (research/advanced-books.md §2). Nothing here describes intimate pastimes; NOD 34 governs the page.

## B. Why this topic belongs at this level

Builds on trd-26, which taught the Lord's energies and Lord Caitanya's acintya-bhedabheda; this page applies that philosophy to its most
important case: the Lord and His pleasure potency, "one and the same, but They have assumed two bodies" (CC Adi 4.56). Builds on trd-32, which
taught why the Lord appeared; here the inner reason, "To accept ecstatic love" (4.53), is traced to Radharani's love (4.49, 4.99–100). Builds on
trd-44, which placed Her at the head of the gopis (4.48–49); here She is studied in Herself.

A resident needs this page because Radharani is everywhere in his life and seldom explained. He bows to Her every morning, sings Her names, and
hears Her glorified in every class; yet asked by a guest "Is Radha a goddess like Laksmi, or Krishna's wife, or what?", he often has only a
sentiment. Prabhupada gave the siddhanta in full in Adi 4, and his language is exact: potent and potency, hladini, mahabhava, "the greatest
servitor". Lower batches hear that Radharani is Krishna's greatest devotee and that we approach Him through Her (VL3, SPS); no lower batch studies
the theology of the pleasure potency.

## C. Central message

"Śrīmatī Rādhikā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa's love. She is His internal energy called hlādinī" (CC Adi 4.59). Krishna's one spiritual
energy has three aspects, sandhini (existence), samvit (knowledge) and hladini (bliss) (4.61–62); "The essence of the hlādinī potency is love of
God, the essence of love of God is emotion [bhāva], and the ultimate development of emotion is mahābhāva" (4.68), and "Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī is the
embodiment of mahābhāva" (4.69). "Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one and the same, but They have assumed two bodies. Thus They enjoy each other, tasting the
mellows of love" (4.56), as inseparable as fire and heat, musk and its scent (4.97). Her name means that She excels all in worship of Krishna
(4.87), and so She "is the medium transmitting the living entities' service to Śrī Kṛṣṇa" (4.56 purport). The resident worships Her, chants Her
name first, and seeks Her mercy, with this understanding.

## D. Learning outcomes

Participants should be able to:
1. Explain from CC Adi 4.55–57 and 4.96–98 how Radha and Krishna are one and two, with Prabhupada's analogies (fire and heat; musk and scent) and the philosophy he names (4.56 purport).
2. Name the three internal potencies and their essences (4.61–67) and trace hladini to prema, bhava and mahabhava (4.68–69).
3. Give the meaning of five of Her names from the Brhad-gautamiya-tantra verse (4.83–98: devi, krsna-mayi, Radhika, parama-devata, sarva-laksmi, sarva-kanti).
4. State why devotees seek Her mercy and chant Her name first (4.56 purport; 4.87 purport; 4.89), and what it means that She is "the greatest servitor".
5. Explain from 4.68 purport that our material madness is the same pleasure potency adulterated, and what that implies for sadhana; and answer a guest's question about Radha accurately in two sentences, within NOD 34.

## F. Philosophical explanation

1. **Potent and potency.** Krishna is sakti-man, the possessor of energy; Radharani is His internal sakti (CC Adi 4.56 purport). Vedanta allows no
   difference between the two, as none between fire and heat (4.56 purport, 4.97); yet "They have assumed two bodies" (4.56) and "exist eternally as
   separate entities" (Bs 5.37 purport). Only Lord Caitanya's acintya-bhedabheda holds both (4.56 purport; trd-26). Why two? "to enjoy rasa" (4.57):
   love is reciprocation "between two" and "cannot be located simply within one's self" (4.60 purport). Hence brahmananda is not prema.
2. **Three potencies, one energy.** Sandhini (existence), samvit (knowledge), hladini (bliss) are one spiritual energy in three functions (4.61–62).
   Krishna's abode, parents and paraphernalia are suddha-sattva, the essence of sandhini (4.64–65); knowledge that Krishna is the Supreme is the essence
   of samvit (4.67); love of God is the essence of hladini (4.68).
3. **From hladini to Radha.** Hladini's essence is prema; prema's essence is bhava; bhava's summit is mahabhava; "Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī is the embodiment
   of mahābhāva" (4.68–69). Ramananda Raya says the same: mahabhava "is represented by Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī", whose body "is a veritable transformation
   of love of Godhead" (CC Madhya 8.160, 8.162). She is "as fully spiritual as Kṛṣṇa" (4.71 purport).
4. **Source of all consorts.** As Krishna is the source of the avataras (trd-31), Radha is the source of the goddesses of fortune, the queens and the
   gopis (4.74–79); "Without many consorts, there is not such exultation in rasa" (4.80). Lalita, Visakha and the others are "expansions of Her
   spiritual body" (CC Madhya 8.165). Even Yasoda is "an expansion of Kṛṣṇa's hlādinī potency" (SB 10.9.20 purport): the one pleasure potency acts in
   every rasa and is embodied fully in Radha.
5. **Her names.** The Brhad-gautamiya verse (4.83) is unpacked: devi, resplendent and the abode of Krishna's worship (4.84); krsna-mayi, She sees
   Krishna everywhere and is identical with Him (4.85–86); Radhika, "Her worship [ārādhana] consists of fulfilling the desires of Lord Kṛṣṇa" (4.87),
   as the gopis themselves said when they saw Her footprints beside His (SB 10.30.28; 4.88); parama-devata, "worshipable for everyone", "the mother of the
   entire universe" (4.89); sarva-laksmi, source and sum of all fortune and of Krishna's six opulences (4.90–91); sarva-kanti, all beauty rests in Her and
   "All the desires of Lord Kṛṣṇa rest in Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī" (4.92–94).
6. **Why we approach Krishna through Her.** Two purports give the reason without sentiment. She is "the greatest servitor" (4.87 purport): the name Radha
   itself means the one who worships best. And She "is the medium transmitting the living entities' service to Śrī Kṛṣṇa. Devotees in Vṛndāvana therefore
   seek the mercy of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī in order to be recognized as loving servitors of Śrī Kṛṣṇa" (4.56 purport). Hence "Hare" before "Krishna" in the
   maha-mantra, Radha-Krishna on the altar, and the acharyas' prayers to Her.
7. **The same potency, adulterated.** "When the same hlādinī-śakti is adulterated by the external, material energy and emanates from the living being, it
   does not attract Kṛṣṇa; on the contrary, the living being becomes attracted by the glamor of the material energy" (4.68 purport). The resident's old
   madness for the world and his new madness for Krishna are one potency facing two ways. Sadhana does not kill the capacity for love; it returns it.
8. **The chapter's end is the Lord's appearance.** "Her love is the cause of Lord Kṛṣṇa's tasting the sweetness of the conjugal relationship" (4.49), and
   so "Kṛṣṇa appeared as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya with the mood and complexion of Śrī Rādhā" (4.99–100): Radha and Krishna "in one body" (4.57). Radha-tattva
   and Gaura-tattva are one study (trd-32).
9. **How a resident speaks of Her.** As the acharyas do in the open texts: Her position (potency, mahabhava, greatest servitor), Her names, Her qualities
   (compassion, youth, lustre, shyness: 8.166 purport). Not Her intimate pastimes (NOD 34; trd-44). To a guest: "Radha is Krishna's own energy of love and
   His greatest devotee; devotees worship Her with Him and ask Her to recommend them to Him" (4.56 and 4.87 purports).
10. **Misunderstandings.**
    - *Radha is a goddess like Durga or Laksmi.* She is Krishna's internal potency, "the central figure for all the goddesses of fortune" (4.83), their source (4.76); Durga is the external energy (trd-26).
    - *Radha and Krishna are two Gods.* "The two are not different, as evidenced by the revealed scriptures" (4.96); fire and heat (4.97).
    - *Radha is simply Krishna, so worshipping Krishna alone is enough.* They "have taken two forms to enjoy the mellows" (4.98); love needs two (4.60 purport); and She is the medium of our service (4.56 purport). Vrindavana's devotees seek Her mercy.
    - *Speaking much of Radharani's pastimes shows love for Her.* The acharyas gave Her tattva openly and Her lila to the qualified; "Her only business is to fulfill all the desires of Kṛṣṇa" (CC Madhya 8.164), and ours is service, not description.
11. **Pitch for TRD.** Walk CC Adi 4.55–100 in order with the purports as the spine, pausing on 4.56, 4.60, 4.68 and 4.87; close with Ramananda Raya's
    verses and the two-sentence answer for a guest.

## I. Core teaching points

1. **One in two, to enjoy rasa.** CC Adi 4.55–57; potent and potency; fire and heat (4.56 purport); love needs two (4.60 purport). *Transition:* what is this potency?
2. **Three functions of one energy.** Sandhini, samvit, hladini (4.61–62); their essences (4.64–68). *Transition:* follow hladini.
3. **Hladini, prema, bhava, mahabhava, Radha.** 4.68–69; CC Madhya 8.160, 8.162; "Love of God" is an epithet for the pleasure potency (4.60 purport). *Transition:* the same potency in us.
4. **Pure and adulterated.** 4.68 purport: our madness for the world and for Krishna are one energy facing two ways. *Transition:* Her expansions.
5. **Source of all consorts.** 4.74–81; Lalita and Visakha Her expansions (8.165); even Yasoda an expansion of hladini (SB 10.9.20 purport). *Transition:* Her names.
6. **Devi, krsna-mayi, Radhika, parama-devata, sarva-laksmi, sarva-kanti.** 4.83–98; the footprints (SB 10.30.28). *Transition:* so why do we approach Him through Her?
7. **The greatest servitor and the medium of our service.** 4.87 purport; 4.56 purport; 4.89. *Transition:* Ramananda Raya's summary.
8. **Her body is made of love.** CC Madhya 8.162–166 and purport: compassion, youth, lustre, shyness. *Transition:* and the chapter's end.
9. **Radha's love brought Lord Caitanya.** 4.49; 4.99–100 (trd-32). *Transition:* the two-sentence answer.

## J. Stories and illustrations

**1. The footprints (SB 10.30.28, as given in CC Adi 4.88 and purport; BBT translation completed by Srila Prabhupada's disciples).** When Krishna
left the rasa dance, the gopis searched for Him in the forest and found His footprints, and beside them the footprints of one gopi. They said:
"Truly the Personality of Godhead has been worshiped by Her. Therefore Lord Govinda, being pleased, has brought Her to a lonely spot, leaving us all
behind." Krishnadasa Kaviraja cites this to show that the Puranas call Her Radhika because "Her worship [ārādhana] consists of fulfilling the desires
of Lord Kṛṣṇa" (4.87). *Lesson:* the Bhagavatam hints Her name through Her worship: the gopis themselves recognised Her by how She served Him.
Told as the verse tells it; nothing added.

**2. Ramananda Raya on Radharani (CC Madhya 8.160–166).** At Kovvur, when Lord Caitanya asked to hear of Radharani, Ramananda Raya said: "The
essential part of love of Godhead is called mahābhāva, transcendental ecstasy, and that ecstasy is represented by Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī" (8.160); "The
body of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī is a veritable transformation of love of Godhead" (8.162); "Her only business is to fulfill all the desires of Kṛṣṇa"
(8.164). He then described Her body in the words of Raghunatha dasa Gosvami's Premambhoja-maranda: bathed "in the morning in the nectar of compassion,
in the afternoon in the nectar of youth, and in the evening in the nectar of luster itself", dressed in silken garments "one of which is Her natural
shyness" (8.166 purport). *Lesson:* the acharyas' way of describing Radharani is to name the qualities of love She is made of; the resident follows them.

**3. The chapter's purpose (CC Adi 4.58, 4.99–100).** Krishnadasa Kaviraja says plainly why he explains Radharani: "Therefore I shall first delineate the
position of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. From that description the glory of Lord Caitanya will be known" (4.58), and he ends: "Kṛṣṇa appeared as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya with
the mood and complexion of Śrī Rādhā" (4.99–100). *Lesson:* Radha-tattva is not a side subject; without it Lord Caitanya cannot be understood (trd-32).

**Illustration (writer's, not scriptural):** the sun and its sunshine cannot be separated, yet no one confuses the two, and we receive the sun only through
its shine. Prabhupada's own analogies are fire and heat, musk and scent (CC Adi 4.56 purport, 4.97), and his conclusion is the point the illustration serves:
She "is the medium transmitting the living entities' service to Śrī Kṛṣṇa" (4.56 purport).

## K. Anticipated questions and objections

1. **"Is Radha a goddess like Laksmi or Durga?"** She is Krishna's internal potency, "the central figure for all the goddesses of fortune" and their source
   (CC Adi 4.83, 4.76); Durga is the external energy (trd-26). She is "worshipable for everyone" (4.89) as Krishna's own energy of love, not as a separate deity.
2. **"If Radha and Krishna are one, why two Deities on the altar?"** "They have assumed two bodies. Thus They enjoy each other" (4.56); love is reciprocation
   "between two" (4.60 purport). The altar shows the truth of the Absolute: one, enjoying in two.
3. **"Why chant 'Hare' before 'Krishna'?"** Because She "is the medium transmitting the living entities' service to Śrī Kṛṣṇa" and devotees "seek the mercy of
   Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī in order to be recognized as loving servitors" (4.56 purport); She is "the greatest servitor" (4.87 purport), and the servant's prayer goes
   first to the one who serves best.
4. **"Does God need someone to give Him pleasure? Is He not complete?"** He is self-sufficient (4.60 purport), and His completeness includes His pleasure
   potency, which is Himself: "the only source of enjoyment for Śrī Kṛṣṇa" is His own energy (4.71 purport). Nothing outside Him pleases Him; Radha is not outside Him.
5. **"How can my lust be the same energy as Radharani's love?"** 4.68 purport: hladini bestowed by Krishna makes a pure lover; hladini "adulterated by the external,
   material energy" and emanating from the living being makes him mad after matter. The capacity is one; the direction differs. That is why sadhana works.
6. **"May I describe Radharani's pastimes in a class to increase devotion?"** Give Her tattva (this page) and Her qualities as the acharyas give them (8.166 purport);
   leave the pastimes to the Bhagavatam heard in the devotees' company (NOD 34; trd-44). "Her only business is to fulfill all the desires of Kṛṣṇa" (8.164); ours is service.

## L. Case studies

**Case 1 (Invented case for discussion).** A new devotee, raised in a family that worships Durga, asks a resident why the temple "has a goddess beside
Krishna" and whether he must now worship two Gods. The resident, unsure, says "Radha is like Krishna's wife". *Dilemma:* true enough or misleading?
*Principles:* CC Adi 4.56 and purport (potent and potency, one in two); 4.83 (source of all goddesses of fortune); 4.96–97 (not different; fire and heat);
4.89 (worshipable for everyone). *Questions:* What did the answer leave out? What would Prabhupada's two sentences be? *Analysis:* "wife" invites both the
"two Gods" error and the material picture; the siddhanta is potency and potent, one and two, and Her position as the greatest devotee. *Takeaway:* give the
tattva in Prabhupada's words; it is simpler than the sentiment and it travels anywhere.

**Case 2 (Invented case for discussion).** A brahmacari troubled by lust reads CC Adi 4.68 purport and concludes that his desire is "really spiritual" and
need not be fought. *Dilemma:* has he understood the purport? *Principles:* 4.68 purport in full ("adulterated by the external, material energy", "attracted by
the glamor of the material energy"); CC Adi 4.165 (bss-19); SB 10.33.39 purport (faithful hearing reduces lust). *Questions:* Which direction is the potency
facing in him now? What turns it? *Analysis:* the purport says the adulterated potency "does not attract Kṛṣṇa"; it is the same energy facing the wrong way, and
the remedy is not acceptance but redirection by hearing, chanting and service. *Takeaway:* 4.68 purport is hope, not permission.

## M. Discussion and reflection

**M.1 Closing check questions**
1. Who is Radharani in one sentence of Prabhupada's? ("Śrīmatī Rādhikā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa's love. She is His internal energy called hlādinī": CC Adi 4.59)
2. How are Radha and Krishna one and two, and which philosophy holds both? (one as fire and heat; two bodies to enjoy rasa; Lord Caitanya's inconceivable oneness and difference: 4.56 purport, 4.97)
3. Name the three internal potencies and the essence of hladini. (sandhini, samvit, hladini; love of God, whose essence is bhava, whose summit is mahabhava: 4.62, 4.68)
4. What does the name Radhika mean, and which Bhagavatam verse hints it? (Her worship consists of fulfilling Krishna's desires; SB 10.30.28, the footprints: 4.87–88)
5. Why do devotees seek Her mercy? (She is the greatest servitor and the medium transmitting our service to Krishna: 4.87, 4.56 purports)
6. What happens to the hladini potency when it is adulterated? (it emanates from the living being, does not attract Krishna, and the being is attracted by the glamour of matter: 4.68 purport)

# Part 2: For students

## S1. Summary

To explain one verse of Svarupa Damodara, Krishnadasa Kaviraja first explains Srimati Radharani, "From that description the glory of Lord Caitanya will
be known" (CC Adi 4.58). The verse says that the loving affairs of Radha and Krishna are manifestations of the Lord's internal pleasure-giving potency, and
that "Although Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in Their identity, They separated Themselves eternally" (4.55). "Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one and the same, but They have
assumed two bodies. Thus They enjoy each other, tasting the mellows of love" (4.56). Srila Prabhupada explains: Krishna is the potent, Radharani the potency,
and the two are no more separable than fire and heat; only Lord Caitanya's philosophy of inconceivable oneness and difference can hold this (4.56 purport).

Krishna's one spiritual energy has three functions: sandhini (existence), samvit (knowledge) and hladini (bliss) (4.61–62). "Śrīmatī Rādhikā is the
transformation of Kṛṣṇa's love. She is His internal energy called hlādinī" (4.59), which "gives Kṛṣṇa pleasure and nourishes His devotees" (4.60). "The
essence of the hlādinī potency is love of God, the essence of love of God is emotion [bhāva], and the ultimate development of emotion is mahābhāva" (4.68),
and "Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī is the embodiment of mahābhāva" (4.69). Prabhupada adds that when this same potency is "adulterated by the external, material
energy" it makes the living being mad after matter instead of after Krishna (4.68 purport). She is the source of all Krishna's consorts, as He is the
source of all incarnations (4.76); Lalita, Visakha and the other gopis are expansions of Her body (CC Madhya 8.165).

Her names are explained from a verse of the Brhad-gautamiya-tantra (4.83). She is krsna-mayi, who sees Krishna everywhere (4.85); Radhika, because "Her worship
[ārādhana] consists of fulfilling the desires of Lord Kṛṣṇa" (4.87), as the gopis recognised when they saw Her footprints beside His (SB 10.30.28); parama-devata,
"worshipable for everyone" (4.89); sarva-laksmi and sarva-kanti, the source of all fortune and beauty (4.90–94). "Lord Kṛṣṇa enchants the world, but Śrī Rādhā
enchants even Him" (4.95). She is "the greatest servitor" (4.87 purport) and "the medium transmitting the living entities' service to Śrī Kṛṣṇa", which is why
devotees seek Her mercy (4.56 purport) and chant "Hare" first. Ramananda Raya told Lord Caitanya that Her body "is a veritable transformation of love of
Godhead" and that "Her only business is to fulfill all the desires of Kṛṣṇa" (CC Madhya 8.162, 8.164). The chapter ends: Krishna "appeared as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
with the mood and complexion of Śrī Rādhā" (4.99–100).

## S2. Key ideas
- Radha and Krishna are one and two: potency and potent, fire and heat, two bodies to enjoy rasa (CC Adi 4.55–57, 4.96–98).
- One internal energy, three functions; hladini's essence is love of God, whose summit, mahabhava, is Radharani (4.61–69).
- The same potency adulterated makes us mad after matter; returned to Krishna it makes us His lovers (4.68 purport).
- She is the greatest servitor and the medium of our service; hence "Hare" first and Her mercy sought (4.87, 4.56 purports).
- Lord Caitanya is Krishna with Her mood and complexion (4.99–100).

## S3. Verses to remember
- **CC Adi 4.59:** "Śrīmatī Rādhikā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa's love. She is His internal energy called hlādinī." For me: who She is, in one line.

## S4. Words to know
- **Hladini-sakti:** the Lord's internal pleasure potency; Radharani is its embodiment (CC Adi 4.59–60).
- **Sandhini, samvit, hladini:** the three functions of Krishna's one spiritual energy: existence, knowledge, bliss (4.62).
- **Mahabhava:** the highest development of love of God; Radharani is its embodiment (4.68–69).
- **Acintya-bhedabheda:** Lord Caitanya's philosophy of inconceivable oneness and difference (4.56 purport).
- **Aradhana:** worship; the root of the name Radha (4.87 purport).

## S5. Questions and answers
1. **Is Radha a goddess like Laksmi or Durga?** No. She is Krishna's own internal energy and the source of all goddesses of fortune (CC Adi 4.83, 4.76); Durga is the external energy. She is worshipable for everyone as Krishna's own energy of love (4.89).
2. **If Radha and Krishna are one, why two Deities?** They "have assumed two bodies. Thus They enjoy each other" (4.56); love is reciprocation between two (4.60 purport).
3. **Why do we chant "Hare" before "Krishna"?** Because She is the greatest servitor (4.87 purport) and the medium transmitting our service to Krishna; devotees seek Her mercy to be recognised as His servants (4.56 purport).
4. **Does God need someone to please Him?** He is self-sufficient, and His own pleasure potency, which is Himself, is "the only source of enjoyment for Śrī Kṛṣṇa" (4.71 purport). Nothing outside Him pleases Him; Radha is not outside Him.
5. **How is my lust related to Radharani's love?** It is the same pleasure potency "adulterated by the external, material energy" (4.68 purport). Sadhana turns it back toward Krishna; it is not a licence to keep it as it is.
